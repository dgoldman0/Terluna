## Next work

Start with manageable global topography for elevation-area/volume curves and connected-basin filling. Specify a reference gravitational/equipotential surface before treating a height contour as a water level. Compare concentrated deep basins with more distributed water under an identical inventory. A static inundation map is distinct from rainfall, runoff, erosion and persistent freshwater supply.

Couple the alternative basin geometries to [climate](../climate/) and elemental/water accounts in [biosphere](../biosphere/). Retain multiple candidate geographies so the core can describe a whole world rather than one landscape.

Older atlas, mist-highland and inland-sea descriptions remain hypotheses until their water and climate budgets are established. See [research status](../research/status.json).
