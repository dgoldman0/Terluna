Model and uncertainty boundary

The full reference traces gas and cloud paths throughout the 600-km spherical atmospheric domain, including light that scatters out of the cloud and returns. The cloud is a finite frozen 97 × 65 × 97 grid. Its source density, morphology, particle phase function and the atmospheric profile remain specified inputs. Ground and space boundaries are black, with collimated solar illumination. Aerosols, polarization, refraction, finite-disk effects and cloud dynamics are excluded. Standard errors describe random sampling only. They do not bound voxel, crop, atmospheric or particle-model error.

The shoreline runtime remains unchanged. Source, algorithms, failed inherited-A2 agreement checks and reproduction commands are recorded in A3_METHODS.md. All displayed evidence is embedded; no network connection is required.
