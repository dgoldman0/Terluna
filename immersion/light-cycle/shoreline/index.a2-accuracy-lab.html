Still open: integrating A2 atmospheric transport into the shoreline renderer; comparing production cloud lighting against a coupled gas+cloud reference; cache/interpolation calibration; cloud-grid refinement beyond 97 × 65 × 97; entraining mixed-phase parcel physics; conserved condensate and precipitation.
