Model boundaries

The molecular sky retains the preceding spherical spectral calculation and its prescribed exponential profiles. This release repacks its data; the 4.6% energy-accounting discrepancy and uncertainty in deep twilight remain unresolved.

Cloud density, rain forcing, wind, humidity, and temperature form an authored four-hour episode. Cloud optical depth is derived from selected water content, layer thickness, and droplet radius. Cloud illumination and multiple scattering remain approximate.

Water stores, overflow, evaporation, and drainage are tracked in representative exposed, canopy, and sheltered columns. Terrain, vegetation, building design, sound, and wave amplitudes are illustrative. Human low-gravity gait and plant viability are outside this model.

The solar path is equatorial and zero-declination. Refraction, Earthlight, star fields, realistic orbital ephemerides, and physiological eye adaptation remain future work. The Earth reference reuses the same illustrative local scene.

Sources and licensing

Three.js r180, MIT license. Rendering uses its scene graph, mesh instancing, PBR materials, shadows, and environment-map filtering.

The inherited sky inputs and their hashes are preserved in the source package. Their methods cite Bruneton’s precomputed atmospheric-scattering work and Wyman, Sloan & Shirley’s colour-matching approximations.

OPEN MOON / SHORELINE

Explore the shoreline

Walk through the light, step under the rain shelter, and watch the landscape stay wet after the sky clears.

Look and walkDrag to look. Use W A S D to walk, Shift to move faster, and Space for a small ballistic hop. On touch screens use the left joystick.
Visit four placesShoreline, shelter, woodland path, and overlook are quick jumps within the same three-dimensional scene.
Compare conditionsHold noon fixed and choose Rain, Clearing, or After rain in Conditions. The month slider changes the Sun separately.
Listen and watchSound follows the shore, trees, and shelter. Live mode advances environmental time; Optical study holds the scenario for inspection.

The sky is inherited model output. The terrain and weather episode are explicit illustrative scenarios. This build’s complete WebGL path awaits browser verification; the source package records the checks actually performed.
