# The vacuum economy's scoping study

This renderer reads the period model's scoping product, [results/scoping.json](../../research/studies/vacuum_economy_periods/results/scoping.json), and draws the seven figures of the [scoping study](../../research/studies/vacuum_economy_periods/scoping_study.md): residents under the base case, in dollars alone and on the programme's ramp; what the settlements pay Earth and receive from its customers, by kind; U's rate against its floor and its reserve against what the run test asks; Earth's holdings of U by group; the funders' cumulative position; residents by 2341 across demand and technology; and the change in residents when one input is moved. It then builds the study's text with them into a PDF through pandoc and xelatex, with [header.tex](header.tex) setting its typography and title page.

Run `PYTHONPATH=. python3 visualization/vacuum-economy-periods/report.py` from the repository root; `--figures-only` stops before the PDF. The figures, the PDF and a manifest go to the ignored `research/runs/vacuum_economy_periods/report/`. The manifest records the product's, the renderer's and the text's hashes and every plotted value, the series at ten-year steps. The renderer calculates nothing: every value is read from the product, which [scoping.py](../../research/studies/vacuum_economy_periods/scoping.py) draws from the model and its saved results, and the study's tests check that the product is current with them. The build needs pandoc, xelatex, TeX Gyre Pagella and Inter.
