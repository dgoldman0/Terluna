What the globe shows

Computed. Land and sea, and the depth of every sea and lake, come from the geography atlas product: LOLA topography above the GRAIL geoid at 4 pixels per degree, filled to one common sea level. Heritage sites and sampling targets come from the conservation study's register.

Display choices. Water is coloured by depth band. Land is a neutral grey placeholder until the biosphere and climate work supply surface cover. The light follows the viewer so every face reads as a map. Relief is exaggerated by the slider, and the seas stay flat at sea level.

Next refinements. Groundwater in the porous crust and crustal loading move the shoreline. Each catchment's rainfall and evaporation set which closed depressions hold lakes; here every depression below sea level is filled.
