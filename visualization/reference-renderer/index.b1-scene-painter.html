How to read these frames

The exposure scale is shared across the sequence. Twilight will be dim at 0 EV; raising exposure reveals colour without changing the simulated light. Raw samples remain available through the filter toggle. Mottling can come from sampling noise as well as cloud structure. The filter averages neighbouring pixels in linear light and can soften cloud edges. The sequence consists of frozen lighting states; it does not simulate evolving weather or an orbital time line.

What is still simplified

The coast and material spectra are authored reference choices. Water is smooth and optically deep: Fresnel-reflected light is traced; transmitted light is absorbed. There are no waves, seabed, individual trees, aerosols, eye adaptation or photographic glare. Raised and thicker clouds are controlled optical transformations of the fair-weather field, not newly solved weather systems. Sampling, spectral grouping, geometry and cloud-domain convergence remain separate open gates.
