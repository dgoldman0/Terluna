Rewrite its active work order from the integrated evidence, while preserving historical sources. The main README likewise has some shorter summaries older than its detailed status and domain records. ### What is adopted and what remains conditional

The branch decision register explicitly adopts the new dawn/dusk definition, measured Earth brightness and phase curve, the study's order and four coasts, use of cloud microphysics' own ice sizes with a 140-micrometre check, and living seas represented by labelled design guesses. It does not establish the guessed concentrations or bioluminescence as predicted biology.

The 140-micrometre distinction is material to electricity: saved CM1 cloud fields may have developed with radiation that capped crystal sizes even when microphysics produced larger ice. Rendering the microphysical sizes consistently does not retroactively correct that weather's radiative history.

The original 24 full path-traced frames remain deferred in the study's work order. Later supplemental images and the built renderer do not make that whole original set complete. The supplemental photographic illustrations preserve generated candidates, prompts, computed references and residual differences. A rejected clipped-white Earth disk and the failed first public sky page remain historical findings; neither should become the preferred representation again. No images were newly reviewed in this analysis.

### The two calendars have distinct meanings

The older sea calendar uses the matched 7 February–8 March 2038 shore month. The portable date calendar is a later implementation with finite spectral sources and separately retained validation. Its historical sea-calendar comparison differs by up to 1.816% in daylight and 2.335% in deep night. Keep both calculations with their identities; don't rewrite old scene values from the new calendar without redoing the scene and its provenance.

The portable calendar supports 2000–2500 inclusive. It takes TT as the orbital timescale and assumes TT−UTC = 69.184 seconds after the recorded leap seconds. The saved JPL comparison covers 5,021 rows at 5,010 epochs, with maximum sampled direction differences of 0.0592° for Sun and 0.0559° for Earth. Ordinary tested centre crossings differ by up to 7.48 minutes; near-grazing brightness crossings can differ by about an hour. Numerical event root precision is not astronomical accuracy, and sampled agreement is not a uniform 500-year bound.

For eclipses, the product flags overlap but retains unobscured light because atmospheric shadow transport is unsolved. The public sky's separate Earth exposure, glare bloom, colour desaturation and roughly 7° display sea facets are display approximations. They must not become physical inputs to illumination, ecology, or navigation.

### How sea informs the other branches

Infrastructure gains location/date-specific daylight and Earthlight, a firmer distinction between twilight and darkness, coastal terrain/water context, and a visual-comfort starting point. Long twilight affects scheduling and night aviation; it does not prove visibility through fog or safe crown operations. Its old five-hour schedule assumptions need explicit correction.

Electricity gains independent use of the same condensate fields and their native ice sizes. Its aerosol profiles could later quantify missing haze and contrast loss in the sea scenes. At present the radiance solver has not consumed those aerosol profiles, and the calendar contains no lightning light. Neither addition is achieved by merging files.

Shield work gains a clear surface-light interface that its 5% dimming and future optical geometry must respect. Earthlight does not pass through the solar shield in the calendar. A more detailed shield/upper-air result does not automatically require regenerating the unchanged lower-atmosphere sky column. An explicit changed input or changed physical scenario is the trigger.

Biology gains conditional underwater light and a reason to distinguish far-side twilit coasts from deep-dark equatorial settings. A 2.98-lux visual threshold is not a photosynthesis threshold. Day/night biological energy must use spectral photon flux and a full light history.

### Integration hazards

1. `research/status.json` is byte-identical to main and still dated 4 October; it has no sea-appearance or portable calendar update. A clean Git merge will leave the work invisible there unless deliberately reconciled.
2. Preserve all sea-side additions to `Makefile`, constants exports, Earthlight consumers, immutable input manifests and source credits. Preserve main's constants-by-value rule. Shield also edits the constants files, and automatic combination is only a syntactic result.
3. Shield changes solar GM from `1.32712440018e20` to `1.3271244004127942e20`. The calendar's recorded constants do not use `SUN_GM`; `geography/tides.py` does. Handle the tide's original constants hash as historical provenance; do not globally regenerate all optics just because the constants file changed.
4. A temporary sea-plus-shield tree audit matched all recorded calendar producer-file hashes: 3 astronomy, 37 transfer including optical files, and 5 validation entries. No changed solar-GM constant occurs in the recorded calendar constant lists. This is encouraging compatibility evidence, not an all-branch build or external-input validation.
5. Ignored runtime products are essential. `research/runs/sea_appearance`, `waves`, and `optical_comfort` link to the research drive. The light-calendar cache, cloud transport arrays, admitted terrain rows, spectra, Earth textures and HDR-VDP/Octave inputs require separate restoration. Git alone does not supply them.
6. The normal calendar build can succeed with a working almanac but missing sky assets. For a usable public sky, acceptance must include `python visualization/light-calendar/build.py --require-assets`, not merely a green default `make check`.
7. Source instructions generally exclude generated visualization pages/images, while sea deliberately commits reviewed image artifacts and their provenance. Retain the branch's authored evidence bundle and existing ignore rules; do not expand it to unrelated generated dist files or delete the review images as routine cleanup.
