# The joint integration of 21 November 2026: the vacuum buildout's economy and the atlas round

Five states of the work became one on 21 November 2026: main, at `58286a18` when the round was called and at `2257668e` with the register's row on it, the vacuum economy's branch at `97177c89`, resources at `346e17f0`, hydrosphere at `1baa2658` and geography at `f1b27235`. The merges ran on the standing integration branch `maintenance/integration` in the worktree `../terluna-integration`, fast-forwarded first from the eighth round's head, `4b31673f`, to main. Each branch's merge is an ordinary merge commit by exact head, in the order economy, resources, hydrosphere, geography: resources' salt study reads the economy's source-access product, and the other two read neither.

The round takes in all the work beyond main, the economy's with it, on the round's reading of the register's row of 21 November ([register](../decisions.md#the-atlas-round-and-the-vacuum-economy-closed-for-now-and-the-ninth-integration-21-november-2026)). The register's condition of 15 November is that main takes none of the refactored economy until the author has seen the report of the refactoring. The register's rows of 17 and 18 November record the author reading the report's PDF on 17 November and directing its rewriting; no row records the rewrite of 18 November read. The decision of 21 November names the economics among the studies to close ahead of the integration. Main moves only at the author's word, where the reading is confirmed or corrected. Both studies were closed for now before the round, each with a section in its README that says where it stands and what is open: [the atlas round](../studies/atlas_round/README.md#where-the-round-stands-21-november-2026) and [the period model of the economy](../studies/vacuum_economy_periods/README.md#where-the-study-stands-21-november-2026).

`domain/provisioning` (`adeaa8ea`) and `domain/humanities` (`c077b606`) come in inside the economy's branch, which merged them on 15 November with their history kept, as do the eight research branches made during the returns work, consolidated-returns, credit-supported-expansion, export-strategies, price-pressure, staged-complex-returns, u-monetary-pressure, u-policy-risk and u-trade-transition, whose commits all lie inside `domain/humanities`. Atmosphere, climate, ecology, food-systems, geology, array-industry and Jura Island stood at main's head of 16 November with no work of their own.

The round has no synthesis study and no joint computation. The economy's settlements are those of the vacuum period, before the water, and no product of one study reads the other's; the one place they touch is named under what is still to do.

## The review

An independent reviewer read the merged tree at the records commit, `16c842b3`, without changing it, before main moved (its brief and findings on the research drive, `session-tools/integration9_review/`). It found nothing blocking. It confirmed that the four merges are ordinary merges by exact head and that the three without conflicts alter nothing of their branches; that the ten conflicted files equal the economy's branch's and the salt product differs from resources' in one recorded digest; that the 35 attributed commits are the ones without a co-author line; that every quotation in the register's 28 new rows and in the row of 21 November is exact, with no Decision claiming more than the words; that the test figures agree with the log; and, on a sample of 40 values and four whole products, the digest check's counts. It judged the reading that the economy comes in a fair one. The answer:

- **Counts.** The completeness check flags six pointers and two pins, five and two of them on main before; the atlas round's grids on the drive carry 137 recorded digests; the industry runs' folder is 1.1 GB; 27 of the 28 new statuses name a commit.
- **The digest check on the branches.** Geography's and the economy's heads carry the merged tree's 107 between them; resources' head carries others, which the merged tree does not take.
- **The condition of 15 November** is given in its own terms, with what the register records of the author seeing the report, and the economy's coming in is marked here as the round's reading, as the register's row has it.
- **Attribution.** The three merges name 34 commits and the one on main is attributed by the register and this account; the register's status no longer says the eighth round's account holds the list.
- **Still to do** now carries the review of the economy's nine commits already on main, the differing digests with the register of sources that no longer exists, the links main's checkout needs, and the oxygen run's hours.
- **Smaller points.** Where the 34 other digests sit, the digests that name files inside a checkout, the period model's row in the research index, and two of the new rows' Decisions brought closer to their words.

The reviewer did not run the suite, rerun the salt producer or read the substance of the two studies, and the complete suite ran on the last merge, `e6759adb`, before the records.
