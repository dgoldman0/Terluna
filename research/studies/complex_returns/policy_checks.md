## Research and substantive review

The study is a conditional planning analysis for the project author and potential
financial reviewers. Its purpose is to compare financing and payment resilience
without treating interest transfers, new claims or resident spending as whole-zone
social returns. Shared scholarly and professional writing guidance was applied.

Two bounded research reviews examined official mechanisms and source accounts.
The accounting review identified outside-lender loss attribution and the need to
exclude existing foreign debt service from supplier-U acceptance. Both distinctions
were corrected and covered by tests. A subsequent synthetic review confirmed the
corrections and sampled real-path ledger identities. The producer's full numerical
checks and substantive reading remain distinct activities.

Official ECB transmission sections, Bank of England facility and risk-management
sections, and the BIS official summary were inspected. The BIS full-PDF retrieval
failed; full-paper reading remains unresolved and is not claimed. The exact source
scope is recorded in policy_sources.json.

The write-up distinguishes fixed-schedule rate relief from actual investment
reruns, dated monthly stress from long-run forecasts, and borrower interest savings
from the costs of delayed payouts and investment. It preserves U's meaning and
the established pre-Open-Moon service boundary. Tables and examples were checked
against the delivered JSON product; no rendered PDF or separate plot was produced.
