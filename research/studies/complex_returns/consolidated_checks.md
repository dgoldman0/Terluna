## Research and substantive review

The study is an expert planning analysis for the author and financial reviewers,
written under the shared scholarly and professional profiles. Two bounded reviews
checked current source accounts and appraisal methods. They identified the need
to separate sponsor incidence from all-stakeholder returns, make saving release
explicit, remove embedded resident payroll before charging opportunity earnings,
and remove bootstrap resource provisions from socioeconomic resource costs.
These distinctions are implemented and tested.

Targeted sections of HM Treasury's Green Book 2026 and MCC's economic and beneficiary
guidance were read. The initially attempted alternative MCC URL returned 404; the
canonical project-recorded page was then retrieved and used. No terrestrial
hurdle rate or causal impact coefficient is imported. The source record states
the reading scope and the unmeasured quantities.

The complete write-up was read for the separation of campaign context, funded
staged capacity, investor incidence, socioeconomic proxies and independent payment
stress. Numerical tables and examples were compared with the product. The result
does not imply that positive staged returns fund completion of the architectural
cap, or that unpriced cultural and knowledge benefits are zero.

## Limits

Numerical reproduction is not empirical validation. Minimum-module engineering,
qualified markets, saving and creditor contracts, resource-cost decomposition,
consumer and research surplus, environmental and heritage losses, payment-event
frequencies and disruption consequences remain unresolved. No default probability,
actual U inflation forecast, complete welfare total or terminal factory value is
assigned. Liquidity episodes do not replace the full long-run production schedule.
