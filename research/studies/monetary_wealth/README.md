# Money, productive assets and ownership in the enclosed economy

Terluna can accumulate substantial productive capacity while much of its financial benefit continues to belong to outside investors. The decisive distinction is between assets located in the off-Earth economy and claims owned by its people and institutions. A large stock of U payment balances answers a third question: how much liquid purchasing power participants choose to hold in the U system.

This study adds annual monetary and wealth accounts to the [staged industrial returns](../enclosed_returns/README.md). It follows nine commercial portfolios under two demand-growth assumptions, with three ownership arrangements for each, from year 0 through year 220. The accounting boundary covers the entire planned off-Earth enclosed economic zone. It ends before Open Moon, the planetary array and outer-system expansion. U retains its established meaning as the general economic unit, potentially one currency or a basket of U-related currencies.

Every amount below is a **constant USD2041 purchasing-power equivalent**. Tables use trillions unless stated otherwise. The money figures value assumed outstanding balances; they establish neither nominal U units nor a U/USD exchange rate. These are conditional accounts, with scenarios for ownership, saving and money holding, rather than measured household wealth or a currency-demand forecast.

## What the optimistic commercial case retains

The worked case is the existing propellant-and-logistics portfolio with breakthrough worldwide demand, a potential audience of two billion, and 1% annual growth in real spending intensity and contract volumes until year 200. Its population reaches the planned 6,468,289 capacity in that year. It preserves the earlier 1,297.979 trillion of cumulative commercial receipts and 347.655 trillion of cumulative owner distributions. Initial funding remains 4.311 trillion, including the initial monetary reserve; the model requires no subsequent equity in this case.

At year 200, surviving equipment and civil structures have a gross replacement value of **59.313 trillion**. An age-adjusted remaining-service calculation gives **34.863 trillion**, with another **4.051 trillion** under construction. Programme cash is **12.363 trillion** and its restricted monetary reserve is **11.780 trillion**. Together these produce an enterprise own-funds proxy of **63.057 trillion**, after debt. The shares that claim these assets are recorded against the enterprise, so adding them does not create a second stock of factories or reserves.

The following comparison uses 15% net saving from identified resident payroll, zero real yield on retained financial balances, and complete retention of community distributions. The paid-purchase case contributes half of that saving to a collective fund and buys shares from year 32 at a disclosed discounted cash-flow quote. The negotiated-transfer case instead transfers 75% of economic ownership gradually during years 33–100 without payment. Both are institutional scenarios; neither follows automatically from investor payback.

| Ownership arrangement | Local economic share | Local net wealth | U money value | Outside cash received | Outside cash NPV at 3% | Outside cash IRR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Continued outside ownership | 0.00% | 0.509 | 15.147 | 347.655 | 11.943 | 6.20% |
| Paid community purchases | 1.27% | 1.056 | 15.022 | 347.909 | 11.947 | 6.21% |
| Negotiated 75% transfer | 75.00% | 285.414 | 24.651 | 110.043 | 6.278 | 5.79% |

The paid-purchase case accumulates 1.056 trillion of local net wealth and obtains 1.27% ownership. Resident payroll is small relative to the enterprise's expected distributable cash, so even sustained saving purchases a limited stake at the 3% quote. The negotiated transfer creates a much larger local claim: its fund retains 237.612 trillion in distributions and owns 47.293 trillion of the enterprise at the year-200 own-funds valuation. Household saving contributes another 0.509 trillion, giving **285.414 trillion** of combined local wealth. Community spending would reduce the retained financial stock; the result measures accumulation under the stated retention policy.

Outside investors still receive **110.043 trillion** in cash under the negotiated transfer, retain 25% of the enterprise, and earn a 5.79% real internal rate of return on dated cash through year 200. Their 3% cash NPV falls from 11.943 to 6.278 trillion. This is a substantial allocation decision: the transfer changes who receives future distributions. It does not increase operating receipts, and the investors' agreement is a condition of the scenario. Share voting rules, essential-infrastructure governance and creditor remedies would require a separate contract.

## Money and financial wealth

The year-200 continued-outside-ownership account contains **15.147 trillion** of domestic-issuer transaction-money value: **11.126 trillion** held by enterprises, **0.255 trillion** by households, **3.477 trillion** retained by outside investors, and **0.289 trillion** held by outside customers for payments. Community holdings are zero in this arrangement. These values arise from explicit liquidity preferences: 90% of programme cash, 50% of household savings, 1% of accumulated outside investor cash receipts, and seven days of U-settled customer purchases. U settlement increases from 25% to 85% over ten commercial years. The community-fund cases keep 5% of their uninvested assets in U transaction balances.

The remaining household and community financial assets are outside-currency portfolio claims. Enterprises keep their remaining operating cash outside the U deposit system. Full sector tables distinguish real assets from financial assets, liabilities and net financial wealth. Financial positions are classified by instrument and denomination, while checkpoint claim lists identify every issuer and holder. Outside actors contain only the counterpart positions needed for this off-Earth account. Their balances are **not complete outside-investor net worth**; investors' contributions and cash receipts are evaluated separately, and the disposition of other outside wealth is unspecified.

Banks hold settlement reserves equal to 10% of their transaction-deposit liabilities. This produces **1.515 trillion** of monetary base in the worked year-200 case. It is not added to transaction money. The separately funded **11.780 trillion** programme reserve is a restricted enterprise claim backed by outside assets at the reserve institution. Its location does not make it an unencumbered public endowment.

The banking account solves outside backing assets as the residual needed to support the selected holdings. Its identity is not an independent balance-of-payments or foreign-exchange availability test. Community ownership of retained bank margin is a separate institutional assumption; the current paths have no material local lending or bank profit.

Annual changes in transaction money reconcile with local loan creation and repayment, changes in outside backing assets, restricted reserve funding, custody liabilities, retained bank income and any outside bank borrowing. Local bank lending creates a deposit paired with a loan; principal repayment extinguishes that deposit liability. Supplier-held U term claims remain financing claims outside the transaction-money measure. The selected 18 financial paths draw effectively no credit at the scale reported here: the largest numerical draw is below one dollar. The U-credit policy label therefore supplies no grounds for inventing a large loan-created money stock.

In these paths, banks and the reserve institution consequently hold substantial outside assets against deposits. The 10% settlement-reserve assumption is not a claim that the remaining deposits are unbacked. Future domestic lending would change the asset mix and must meet the inherited underwriting rules. Neither issuing additional deposits nor exchanging an outside asset for U creates net wealth by itself.

Changing outside investors' retained-U fraction from zero to 5% increases year-200 transaction-money value from **11.670 to 29.053 trillion**, while local net wealth remains **0.509 trillion**. Money holdings can therefore vary substantially without changing productive assets or ownership. An illustrative 50/30/20 split among three U-related constituent currencies allocates this one total and every sector’s U-denominated financial claim and liability. These annual constituent views classify the same claims; a basket accounting unit adds no second money stock. The common weights are a reporting scenario, not measured currency-specific exposures. Constituent institutions, exchange rates and clearing contracts remain unspecified.

These holding scenarios also change the disposition of distributions relative to the earlier spending-pressure exercise. The old price screen is not reinterpreted as an inflation forecast for the new ownership arrangements. A subsequent monetary equilibrium model would need consumption, imports, taxes, currency prices and financial-asset demand to respond jointly.

## Ownership prices and valuation limits

The principal physical valuation is surviving replacement cost adjusted by expected remaining service. The provisioning producer follows all 116 active-equipment and structural component categories, preserves their different lifetimes and inherited replacement quotes, and reconciles additions, retirement, ageing and construction in progress. It does not estimate liquidation proceeds. Imported initial equipment and later locally fabricated replacements can have different costs, so an initial cash outlay need not equal the contemporary replacement value of the surviving establishment.

The principal equity valuation is own funds: included physical capital plus programme cash and reserves, less debt. For comparison, a separate conditional share quote discounts future distributions to year 220 at 3%, with year-220 own funds as the terminal mark. There is no perpetual-growth assumption. Purchases execute only where remaining modelled capital calls are absent; the quote is not a discovered market price or a guarantee that holders will sell. Purchases and gifts follow that year's payout; new-capital dilution precedes it.

The negotiated purchase price materially changes local ownership. In the worked case, using discount rates of **3%, 5% and 7%** gives year-200 purchased shares of **1.27%, 13.42% and 100%**, and local own-funds-based wealth of **1.056, 8.719 and 127.806 trillion**. A separate agreement to sell at own funds also permits full local ownership and 390.428 trillion of local wealth. The own-funds price can be far below the value of future distributions; that comparison is a different bargain with investors, not a demonstrated cheap acquisition opportunity. Lower saving also matters: saving 5% rather than 15% of payroll reduces the 3%-quote purchased share to 0.42%; saving 30% raises it to 2.54%.

At year 200 the 3% cash-claim quote is **113.495 trillion**, compared with own funds of **63.057 trillion**. Marking outside equity at that larger quote, while retaining only the included physical assets, produces a **−49.929 trillion** consolidated residual net worth in the fully outside-owned case. The household still owns 0.509 trillion. This difference reflects the enterprise's equity valuation against an incomplete real-asset valuation; it is not a cash insolvency result. The model preserves the enterprise residual rather than silently flooring it or inventing 50 trillion of intellectual property to make the balance disappear.

A paid share transfer exchanges cash for an existing asset. At consistent prices it creates no wealth at the transaction. A transfer without payment reallocates wealth and is recorded as a capital transfer, with no additional output. Economic rights may be widely shared through a community institution without granting each household a separately counted security. The scenarios accordingly give the collective fund ownership directly and do not issue an additional household claim on the same fund assets.

## Accounting basis and remaining integration

Consolidated off-Earth net wealth equals included nonfinancial assets plus external financial assets minus external liabilities, **including outside equity**. Domestic deposits, loans and equity cancel against their issuing counterparties. This treatment follows the balance-sheet and international-position framework in the [2025 System of National Accounts](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/nationalaccount/snaupdate/2025/2025_SNA_Combined.pdf), chapter 14, and [IMF BPM7](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/data/statistics/bpm6/draft-bpm7-wcv.pdf), paragraphs 7.1–7.6. Monetary-holder and base-money distinctions follow the [IMF Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/data/guides/mfsmcg-final.pdf), chapter 6. The source record identifies the particular editions and sections examined.

The household closure applies net saving only to identified resident payroll, including pre-opening and construction work. It assumes no initial personal wealth, additional household borrowing or inherited financial portfolio. The unretained wage share is assigned to outside consumption and services in this bounded account. Local retail profits, secondary wages, taxes, migration wealth and household distribution within the aggregate remain open. Social services supplied by the establishment are not credited again as household bank balances. Resource provisions already treated as expenditure are not accumulated into a second public fund without a recipient account.

Commercial intellectual property, software, brands, licences, research spillovers and the residual service-package capital remain unvalued. Complete water, air, consumable, cargo and product inventories are also unavailable in the physical export. Their omission means unknown value, not demonstrated zero. The [resource-access product](inputs/source_access.json.gz) establishes catalogue and processing bounds, not permitted mineable reserves. Its frozen product is retained here as an evidence condition; no unqualified mineral stock or protected site receives a financial asset value. Existing physical and data-preservation requirements continue to apply.

The source cash payout is a distribution measure, not audited national-accounts income. It may contain capital withdrawals. These results therefore do not establish GDP, GNI, a current-account balance or a complete saving account. Replacement purchases can form new fixed assets even when the source financing ledger pays them from operating cash; depreciation cannot be deducted again from that cash to manufacture an accrual profit figure.

For integration, the principal policy question is which claims people and institutions acquire, at what price, and with which continuing obligations. Strong exports can support both outside returns and substantial local accumulation, but ownership, funded reserves and spending decisions determine who retains that capacity. The study supplies those accounts separately so that subsequent institutional, fiscal and monetary work can change one mechanism without counting its benefit twice.

## Reproduction

[wealth_inputs.json](wealth_inputs.json) records scenario rules and frozen product identities. [accounts.py](accounts.py) reproduces the existing financial paths and exposes dated funding, loan and payout flows. [model.py](model.py) assigns conditional saving and ownership; [ledger.py](ledger.py) consolidates paired claims. [results/wealth.json.gz](results/wealth.json.gz) contains 54 complete annual paths, the five checkpoints, and 13 sensitivity comparisons. Source identities and reading scope are in [sources.json](sources.json); checks and review are recorded in [checks.md](checks.md).

```sh
python -m research.studies.monetary_wealth.accounts
python -m research.studies.monetary_wealth.run
python -m pytest -q -p no:cacheprovider research/studies/monetary_wealth/tests
python visualization/monetary-wealth/render.py
```

The asset producer remains in the existing provisioning branch. This humanities study reads its frozen product and imports no provisioning implementation. The source access snapshot likewise retains the resources branch's ownership of geological and preservation findings. Figures are generated in ignored `research/runs/monetary_wealth/` and preserve the exact result identity.
