# Monetary and wealth account verification

This study is a conditional research account for the author and cross-domain reviewers integrating the enclosed economy. Its purpose is to separate money, financial claims, productive assets, ownership and dated investor cash. It is not an adopted ownership contract or a forecast of nominal U supply. Numerical accounting checks, source reading, substantive review and figure inspection establish different evidence below.

## Interpretation and source reading

The independent review reproduced the worked year-200 receipts, initial funding, three ownership/wealth/investor-return rows, and purchase-price/saving sensitivities. A first paid purchase changes cash-claim-marked consolidated wealth by zero; its book-marked reduction equals the acquisition premium. A first gifted stake changes local book wealth by the recorded transfer value. The review confirmed that unpriced gifts remain null and that later recapitalization dilutes the initially transferred stakes in weaker cases.

The report distinguishes zero-yield retained saving from a consumption or public-spending policy; location of spending from beneficial ownership; enterprise reserves from an unencumbered public endowment; own-funds equity from discounted cash-claim quotes; and an accounting identity from an independently qualified foreign-exchange source. Outside-investor cash returns include dated contributions, distributions and buyout receipts, but no assumed terminal share sale. Partial outside counterparty positions are not presented as total outside-investor wealth.

The IMF monetary statistics manual, 2025 System of National Accounts pre-edit edition, and BPM7 White Cover were consulted in the targeted sections recorded in `sources.json`. Download identities and reading scopes are explicit. No full-manual reading, complete scholarly literature review or observed lunar monetary behavior is claimed. Primary accounting guidance supports the treatment of claims and consolidation; it does not supply the scenario's saving rate, ownership arrangement, liquidity preference or enterprise valuation.

The README was read against the numerical accounts and reviewed independently for substantive financial interpretation. Initial funding, lifetime cash receipts, asset valuation and annual stocks remain separate quantities. The asset producer's omitted inventories and intangibles remain unvalued rather than silently assigned zero. Source cash distributions are not reclassified as audited income, GDP or national saving.

## Outstanding qualifications

The accounts do not establish nominal U faces, constituent exchange rates, measured money demand, an adopted monetary constitution, ownership or voting contracts, household inequality, a complete public budget, complete intellectual-property and inventory values, qualified source reserves, deployment capability or future customer commitments. Monetary backing is solved residually within the assumed financial holdings; it is not a balance-of-payments forecast. No such missing input has been replaced with a claimed empirical result.
