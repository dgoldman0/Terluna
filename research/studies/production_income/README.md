# Year-200 GDP and GNI of the economic zone served by U

The intended self-financing development paths support annual GDP estimates in the **roughly 5.5–21 trillion USD2041 range across the central 80% intervals**, with substantial differences among portfolios. For the diversified portfolio, the balanced planning distribution has a **9.43 trillion median and a 7.67–20.62 trillion central 80% interval**. Its GNI median is 9.24 trillion with continued outside ownership and 9.30 trillion with 75% local ownership; the corresponding upper tails differ more substantially, reaching 12.89 and 18.52 trillion at the 90th percentile. Ownership changes the allocation of earned income while production remains the same.

These are **subjective, conditional planning distributions**, rather than empirically calibrated probabilities for an economy two centuries ahead. The first comparison selects the paths that financed the completed build in the [revised returns study](../enclosed_returns/revised.md), then varies year-200 sales realization, input prices and accounting estimates. Lower sales or higher costs in these new draws can still produce a year-200 operating deficit. A second comparison includes every admitted market case, assuming any required outside funding arrives. Neither comparison assigns a probability to completing the build.

## Boundary and units

U retains its established meaning as a **general economic unit**, potentially a currency or a basket of U-related currencies, serving the planned economy beyond Earth before Open Moon, the habitat array and outer-system development. The accounting boundary includes its resident enterprises, institutions, households and industrial facilities. Earth is external. The planned population reaches about 6.47 million in year 200; population alone neither defines the producers nor identifies their owners.

All results are annual flows in **trillion constant USD2041 purchasing-power equivalents**. They establish no nominal U exchange rate. GDP measures the represented production of resident units after intermediate consumption. GNI adds earned income from outside the zone and subtracts earned income payable outside it. GNP and GNI coincide in this internally reconciled model, which contains no statistical discrepancy. These annual flows are distinct from outstanding U, accumulated wealth, cumulative sales, investor payouts and discounted project value. The residence and production conventions follow the targeted sections of the [2025 System of National Accounts](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/nationalaccount/snaupdate/2025/2025_SNA_Combined.pdf), particularly paragraphs 3.144–3.146 and 7.92–7.103.

The selected paths comprise all nine breakthrough-demand portfolios at 1% annual real growth, plus propellant/logistics, diversified and the earlier manufacturing mix at 0.5%. Among the latter three portfolios, the conditioned growth weights are two-thirds reference and one-third wider. A path enters this comparison only if the previous account required no equity beyond initial funding through year 200, and had positive operating and post-capital cash at that checkpoint. That historical selection is not repeated after every new year-200 draw. The resulting annual operating-deficit frequencies are approximately 20% for diversified, 18% for propellant/logistics, 19% for worldwide services, 31% for computing and 42% for premium products under the stated priors. They are conditional stress frequencies; reserves and a complete subsequent cash path would determine whether an annual deficit creates a funding problem. The model does not convert them into probabilities of insolvency or completion failure.

Raw-resource offers and worldwide services coincide because the inherited dispatch model declines the additional raw-resource cargo at the quoted Earth delivery price. This equality concerns the admitted orderbook, while geological potential remains a separate quantity.

The 75% local column is a counterfactual **year-200 economic ownership share**, not an assumption that every portfolio's previous negotiated transfer actually reaches that share. The result also supplies the 50% endpoint and separately uses all 54 ownership histories from the [revised wealth study](../monetary_wealth/revised.md). That separate comparison retains the actual purchase shares and dilution in the 18 cases for which those histories exist. For example, combining the two breakthrough propellant cases gives GNI of 6.16 trillion (5.29–7.15) under the modeled community-purchase path and 6.54 trillion (5.54–10.65) under its negotiated transfer. It does not extrapolate those ownership histories into the other 63 market cases.

The relatively low medians arise because the balanced prior assigns substantial weight to limited and global market cases. Their small sales accompany the full residential and industrial support system. Outside owners can incur negative accrued earnings in these cases, so GNI may exceed GDP. This is an allocation of losses under direct-investment accounting, not an additional source of commercial success. Across the nine labels, approximately 77%–96% of this particular weighted set has negative year-200 operating cash after the specified realization and price draws. Those percentages describe the chosen funded-completion distribution, not a forecast of the project's failure rate.

The prior matters much more than sampling precision. For the diversified portfolio, changing only the market and growth weights produces:

| Market prior | GDP | GNI: 75% local ownership |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Restrained | 3.24 (2.43–5.65) | 4.02 (3.17–6.19) |
| Balanced | 3.79 (2.68–9.29) | 4.54 (3.41–9.17) |
| Expansive | 7.97 (3.27–20.76) | 8.03 (4.01–18.62) |

The restrained prior assigns 50%, 35% and 15% to the limited/global/breakthrough markets and the static/reference/wider growth cases, respectively. The expansive prior assigns 10%, 30% and 60% in those same orders. These alternatives deliberately test different beliefs; the research does not identify which is the true distribution. Under the balanced funded-completion prior, differences among market cases account for about 96% of diversified GDP variance. Its GDP mean is 5.33 trillion, its 95th percentile 17.42 trillion and its 99th percentile 21.56 trillion. The skew and separation among market cases make a single fitted normal distribution inappropriate.

The probabilities apply only to the admitted market set. They assign no probability to unmodeled industries, a different technological regime, abandonment, a different population cap, Open Moon or later expansion. Consequently, the upper tail is **not a ceiling on U's economic potential**. Completion and market outcomes would also be related in an adaptive investment strategy; treating the funded-completion weights as unconditional forecasts would lose that relationship.

## From the financial accounts to national accounts

The source financial model records worldwide receipts and cash costs. The accounting consumer separates current purchases from investment, adds represented domestic final services and locally produced capital, and then allocates earned income. The worked propellant/logistics case below uses breakthrough demand, wider growth and the **modes of all accounting assumptions**, including full sales realization. It is a deterministic bridge, not the median of a distribution.

| Annual item | Trillion USD2041 |
| --- | ---: |
| Worldwide sales | 17.748 |
| Resident housing and support services, imputed | 3.264 |
| Locally produced capital, including replacement | 0.197 |
| Imported intermediate goods and services | −8.435 |
| **GDP** | **12.774** |
| Cash compensation inside GDP | 0.064 |
| In-kind resident benefits inside GDP | 3.264 |
| Depreciation estimate inside GDP | 3.092 |
| Net enterprise earnings, before ownership allocation | 6.353 |
| **GNI with outside direct-investment ownership** | **6.420** |
| **GNI with 75% local ownership** | **11.185** |

The main production identity is

`GDP = exports + resident final services + own-account capital output − imported intermediate consumption`.

Imported capital appears equally in gross capital formation and total imports, so it cancels in the expenditure measure. In the worked case, capital formation is 2.526 trillion and total imports 10.764 trillion; adding resident services and exports reproduces GDP. Internal purchases among resident enterprises cancel on consolidation. Household purchases imported from Earth would likewise add equally to consumption and imports. The model does not apply an export multiplier or add intermediate mining, electricity and fabrication sales repeatedly.

Resident services require an explicit valuation because the financial study funds housing, food and supporting infrastructure without separate household invoices. The bridge treats household-use services as benefits in kind, adding equal amounts to production and compensation. It values direct residential capital services plus an uncertain share of core support costs from the least commercially active case at the same population. Work-only facilities remain production inputs. This is an analytical institutional convention, not an adopted employment or public-finance system. The approach follows the distinction between household benefits and work requirements in SNA paragraphs 8.49–8.54; cost valuation includes a stated normal return on relevant capital, following paragraphs 7.102–7.103. It creates no additional cash sales or enterprise profit.

The source cash account charges equipment renewal to operating cash. The GDP bridge recognizes identified long-lived replacements as investment and varies the fraction of repair parts treated as ordinary maintenance. It also varies how much launch cost belongs to capital delivery. Existing asset quotes omit that delivery and an unallocated commercial service package, so depreciation adds the capitalized annual freight and residual package-renewal allowances as explicit endpoint proxies. The inherited physical asset depreciation remains an engineering service-value estimate, with a further uncertainty factor; it is not an observed economic depreciation rate.

Depreciation is retained in both gross measures. It is subtracted when calculating net enterprise earnings attributable to owners. The bridge then applies

`GNI = GDP − external interest − outside ownership share × net enterprise earnings`,

with zero real income on accumulated external financial balances, as in the retained wealth study. Domestic interest cancels within U. Foreign earnings include retained earnings and signed losses; the amount of cash distributed in year 200 is not substituted for them. This follows the direct-investment income treatment in the [OECD's 2025 benchmark definition](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-benchmark-definition-of-foreign-direct-investment-fifth-edition_7f05c0a3-en/full-report/fdi-financial-and-income-transactions_af471eb5.html), paragraphs 230 and 239–243.

**The foreign equity stakes are treated as direct investment.** Dispersed portfolio-equity holdings have different retained-earnings treatment and require a specified dividend account. That institutional alternative is outside the displayed intervals. Positive real returns on identified external assets would also raise GNI without raising domestic GDP; neither those returns nor a nominal currency appreciation are assumed here.

The substantial GNI retained under outside ownership includes depreciation and expensive household services. It is not a forecast of cash salaries, discretionary household spending or welfare. In the worked case, cash compensation is only 0.064 trillion of the 6.420 trillion GNI. Cost-valued services can rise when delivering them becomes more expensive, even though residents receive no better service. The wealth and financial-return accounts remain necessary for judging accumulation, purchasing freedom and ownership alongside national output.

## Assumptions, evidence and remaining coverage

The calculation uses 8,192 draws for each of 81 market cases, with the same draws reused across portfolio and ownership comparisons. The eight marginal distributions below are independent triangular planning priors. Shared sales realization and purchased-service price draws couple sectors within a case; GDP and GNI are calculated together from each draw. Market level and long-run growth are independent within each stated scenario prior, an assumption that remains open to revision.

| Quantity | Minimum | Mode | Maximum | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Fraction of admitted sales realized | 0.75 | 1.00 | 1.00 | Common shortfall within the source orderbook; capacity and buyer budgets never increase |
| Purchased current-service price multiplier | 0.80 | 1.00 | 1.25 | Applies to physical logistics/services and purchased commercial inputs; equipment quotes remain separate |
| Launch cost assigned to capital delivery | 0.25 | 0.65 | 0.90 | Unresolved freight allocation within the existing cash total |
| Repair parts treated as routine maintenance | 0.00 | 0.50 | 1.00 | Remainder treated as capital work |
| Depreciation multiplier | 0.75 | 1.00 | 1.30 | Economic-life uncertainty around the engineering and delivery proxies |
| Local capital-output valuation multiplier | 0.75 | 1.00 | 1.25 | Uncertainty in the inherited internal fabrication quote |
| Core support share serving household final use | 0.50 | 0.80 | 0.95 | Direct residential capital services are included separately |
| Normal annual capital return in service valuation | 0% | 2% | 4% | Cost-valuation allowance; creates no new cash yield |

The minimum and maximum of each triangle are modeling bounds, not established physical or economic limits. Three-quarters of the purchased commercial-input bill is assumed variable with sales realization; the remaining quarter and physical support costs persist. Price changes are valued in the same USD2041 equivalents. These single-year draws do not reconstruct a stochastic two-century investment history or an equilibrium between world prices, demand, U credit and production.

Eighteen cases have a complete exported physical asset account. The other 63 estimate depreciation using annual external and local replacement value multiplied by the median ratio from those exports, 1.275. Leaving each exported case out of that calibration in turn gives relative errors from −11.87% to +13.12% against its engineering estimate. This internal comparison supports a bounded approximation within the existing design family; it does not validate the design against actual industrial lifetimes. The 0.75–1.30 factor adds uncertainty beyond that fitting error. A full cohort export for all cases would improve the account.

The main treatment of resource-protection provisions is an earmarked financial appropriation, pending identification of the recipient and services performed. No GDP is credited merely for transferring or reserving money. A separately reported sensitivity treats the entire provision either as a current domestic levy or as an imported current resource service. For the worked case's 0.418 trillion provision, these alternatives change GNI by +0.418 or zero under outside ownership, and by +0.104 or −0.313 trillion at 75% local ownership. The former changes income allocation without creating production; the latter reduces GDP as well. These are classification limits, not additional benefits.

Worldwide sports, entertainment, research, licenses, professional work, computing and destination accommodation remain in the inherited sales and cost accounts. Their future market size is varied through the admitted orderbooks; the model does not replace those services with the resident population's spending. Locally capitalized research and software, a complete inventory account, domestic retail and secondary service production, production taxes, financial-intermediation services, and a full balance of payments remain incomplete. The model uses zero net product taxes and no inventory change beyond represented own-account capital. Unallocated capital cost allowances receive no production value without evidence of what is produced. Retained-core service restoration is not counted as another sale or as new fabrication.

The revised source-access, reclamation, repair, preservation and data-protection conditions remain inherited requirements. Geological stock probabilities do not become probabilities of GDP, mine permission or successful extraction. The physical products support the admitted schedules under their assumptions; the added account and price draws do not qualify new recovery processes or routes. The study therefore supplies a reproducible distribution for the currently represented economic system, with its largest unresolved institutions and production categories identified alongside the result.

## Products and verification

[income_inputs.json](income_inputs.json) records every probability assumption and pins the admitted financial, physical and wealth products. [extract.py](extract.py) reconciles their year-200 records, and [model.py](model.py) constructs the production, expenditure and earned-income accounts. The [result](results/year200.json.gz) includes means, standard deviations, nine quantiles, cumulative probabilities at stated thresholds, inverse cumulative distributions, paired GDP/GNI correlations, variance decomposition, actual ownership comparisons and the deterministic accounting bridges. It preserves the three priors and every portfolio separately.

The [verification record](checks.md) reports source identity checks, numerical accounting controls, tests, an independent larger sample and inspection of the figures. The largest accounting mismatch across the saved draws is less than 0.02 USD2041. Repeating the distributions with 16,384 draws per case and a different seed changes the 648 checked 10th/50th/90th percentiles by at most 0.68%, with a median relative change of 0.11%. This establishes numerical stability at the displayed scale; it does not calibrate the probabilities.

```sh
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python -m research.studies.production_income.extract
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python -m research.studies.production_income.model
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python -m pytest -q -p no:cacheprovider research/studies/production_income/tests
MPLCONFIGDIR=/tmp/terluna-mpl python visualization/production_income/render.py
```

The renderer reads the saved result and writes interval plots, prior-sensitivity curves and a source-hash manifest under the ignored `research/runs/production_income/figures/` directory. It performs no additional economic calculation.
