# The resort at 301 K across the domains

19 October 2026 · the fourth joint integration. The author set the resort at 301 K on 18 October
([register](../../decisions.md#the-target-climate-17-october-2026)), and the branches' work since 17 October took the
resort's pieces one domain at a time: the lakes and seas, their oxygen and methane
([lake kinds](../lake_kinds/README.md), [the seas' oxygen](../sea_oxygen/README.md),
[the waters' methane](../lake_methane/README.md)); the coasts' and islands' water
([coast water](../coast_water/README.md)); the rain and its sound
([atmosphere/rain](../../../atmosphere/rain/README.md)); and what could ease the shield
([heat levers](../heat_levers/README.md)). This study sets them side by side where they meet and works out what follows
from them together. [run.py](run.py) is arithmetic on committed products
([results/resort_301k.json](results/resort_301k.json), schema `terluna.research.resort-301k/1`); no model runs.

## What it finds

**The waters decide the methane more than the gas pair does.** The domain synthesis's ledger, with biology cutting 90%
of every source but the waters and the waters at the flux the lakes' and seas' methane study traced at 301 K, against
the soils' uptake of 2.92 Tg a year for each ppm, at the methane share's centre and Osborn's rule for the deep mixing:

| The waters, with Earth's methanotrophs | Their methane | The air left to the soils | Removal to hold 1.87 ppm; air through the contactors a year | Removal to hold the levers' low pair, 0.3 ppm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nutrient-poor | 0.57 Tg a year | 1.01–1.75 ppm | none | 2.1–4.2 Tg; 0.39–0.80% |
| Mesotrophic | 7.8 Tg | 3.49–4.23 ppm | 4.7–6.9 Tg; 0.14–0.21% | 9.3–11.5 Tg; 1.76–2.17% |
| Eutrophic | 34 Tg | 12.47–13.21 ppm | 31–33 Tg; 0.94–1.01% | 35.5–37.7 Tg; 6.73–7.14% |
| New reservoirs throughout | 57 Tg | 20.41–21.15 ppm | 54–56 Tg; 1.64–1.71% | 58.7–60.9 Tg; 11.12–11.53% |

The study's own brackets move the nutrient-poor row. At the methane share's 0.8 those waters send 3.57 Tg a year, put
the air at 2.04–2.78 ppm and need 0.5–2.6 Tg a year of removal at the held pair, the contactors at the low pair passing
0.96–1.37% of the air a year; under Shih and Bouffard's form of the deep mixing they send 1.69 Tg, put the air at
1.39–2.13 ppm and need up to 0.77 Tg at the held pair, 0.61–1.01% of the air a year at the low pair. With the
methanotrophs designed for sudden overturns, mesotrophic waters send 4.9 Tg a year and need 1.9–4.0 Tg of removal at the
held pair. The low pair saves 0.79–0.81 points of dimming (heat levers); its methane contactors cost about 0.07 TW for
each 1% of the air passed a year, 0.03–0.06 TW with nutrient-poor waters at the centre and 0.12–0.15 TW with mesotrophic
ones, while the low pair's N2O decomposition, 0.96–1.47 TW, stays its larger cost. Nutrient-poor waters let the held
pair stand with no removal at the centre and with up to 2.6 Tg a year across the brackets; every richer state needs
removal at either pair. The air loses its methane to the soils and, beyond them, to the contactors: at the held pair the
soils take 5.5 Tg a year from an air holding 3,290 Tg, a turnover of about 600 years; at the low pair the contactors
alone pass its methane in 125–255 years, 103–179 with the soils.

**The deep lakes stay nutrient-poor while people send them little dissolved phosphorus, and how little turns on the
giants' floors.** The lakes by kind give each kind's oligotrophic line at 301 K, from its own depth and throughflow: the
self-ventilated lakes, a tenth of the area, take the least, and the giants, 70% of it, the most while their floors stay
oxic and almost none where they go anoxic, when release from the floor cancels their retention. The land's own dissolved
phosphorus brings the lakes 0.017–0.045 g per m² a year. What is left under the lines for people's dissolved phosphorus,
kind by kind, shared among the people on the Moon:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Earth's | 318–346 g; 159–173 g | 88–393% | 37–46 g; 19–23 g | 10–53% |

The plan loses 44–180 g of phosphorus a person a year (the food study and the sea phosphorus screen), against 1.76–2.4
kg in Earth's practice. Most of a field's loss leaves bound to soil, and the deltas keep it, while the vented glass
houses' bleed alone carries 0–90 g of phosphorus a person a year in nutrient solution, which fits the oxic room at the
middle settling with 4 billion and passes it with 8. With the giants' floors oxic, 32–161% of the design loss could
arrive dissolved at the middle settling, all of it with Earth's settling and 9–66% at g/6; with their depths anoxic, the
dissolved part must be kept from the lakes, recovered as struvite or sent to the seas. The trophic state is a tipping
point (lake anoxia): once a deep lake's floor goes anoxic it gives back what the floor held, the methane follows the
table above, and the giants go anoxic at depth when mesotrophic. The mixing's form, which a basin-scale model of one
crater lake would settle, decides between the two cases.

**The seas get almost none of it.** The lakes hold 96% of the land's phosphorus, and on the rivers alone the held fresh
seas reach 0.27–1.1 mg/m³ of dissolved phosphorus at 1,000 years; dust falling on the waters adds to it, to 0.71–1.79
mg/m³ at the inherited dust envelope's low end and 18–28 mg/m³ at its top (sea phosphorus), against Earth's ocean mean
of 70 mg/m³. The author held the seas fresh on the condition that they are rich in life, which the register sets as high
biodiversity, as Earth's old, clear fresh lakes hold hundreds of endemic species through variety of habitat and time,
with phosphorus from the dredged deltas for the open seas if they need more production. On the rivers alone the seas run
ultra-oligotrophic, a question of production for ecology's community design, which the delta lever answers. A
mesotrophic sea would bring its own methane, 4.3 Tg a year, and a deepest tenth of 2.1 mg/L under Osborn's rule and at
most 0.88 mg/L with Shih and Bouffard's form at the two great basins (the seas' oxygen), the cost of that richer state;
the dust envelope's top, 18–28 mg/m³, is itself mesotrophic by the lakes' classes (10–35 mg/m³), so that cost could come
with the dust unchosen.

**The resort's dimming with the levers worth pursuing.** At 301 K the resort's own dimming is 4.5–5.6% of the hot
world's absorbed sunlight and 8.2–10.9% with the gases, the glow and the beamed power answered (the targets). The
levers' first four, the glow's set-aside answers, food made in orbit, the low gas pair and white roofs with silver
foliage, save 1.6–3.0 points. Taken with the case each belongs to, the glow's answers at the glow's efficacy and food
made in orbit at the beams' end, they leave the resort 6.1–8.3% of dimming in all and save 17–31% of it (heat levers);
with nutrient-poor waters at the centre the low pair's methane half costs the contactors under 0.06 TW.

## Assumptions and limits

- The methane budget is the lakes' and seas' methane study's, at its central methane share and Earth's methanotrophs
  unless named, with the share's 0.8 and Shih and Bouffard's form given beside it.
- The phosphorus lines are the lake kinds' at 301 K, by kind, from Vollenweider's relation on each kind's depth and
  throughflow with lake anoxia's three settlings; the room is each kind's own, while people's losses gather near towns
  and farms, which the evenly shared figure does not place. The giants' anoxic case takes every giant at the line of an
  anoxic floor.
- People's dissolved phosphorus is taken as wholly bioavailable; the land's is the sea phosphorus screen's bioavailable
  input.
- The watering scale's lift is each band's groundwater median under the deserts, at the mature water table; from lakes
  or rivers the lift is each garden's height above its water. The albedo cost carries the levers' kernels for the hot
  world's and the lock's deserts, with each band's diurnal mean sunlight, (4/π) cos of the band's middle latitude.
- Nothing here runs a climate model: the deserts' rain under watering, the seas' life and the lakes' chemistry under
  people's loads are for the 301 K design case and its studies.
