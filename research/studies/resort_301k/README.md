# The resort at 301 K across the domains

19 October 2026 · the fourth joint integration. The author set the resort at 301 K on 18 October
([register](../../decisions.md#the-target-climate-17-october-2026)), and the branches' work since 17 October took the
resort's pieces one domain at a time: the lakes and seas, their oxygen and methane
([lake kinds](../lake_kinds/README.md), [the seas' oxygen](../sea_oxygen/README.md),
[the waters' methane](../lake_methane/README.md)); the coasts' and islands' water
([coast water](../coast_water/README.md)); the rain and its sound
([atmosphere/rain](../../../atmosphere/rain/README.md)); and what could ease the shield
([heat levers](../heat_levers/README.md)). This study sets them side by side where they meet and works out what follows
from them together. [run.py](run.py) is arithmetic on committed products
([results/resort_301k.json](results/resort_301k.json), schema `terluna.research.resort-301k/1`); no model runs.

## What it finds

**The waters decide the methane more than the gas pair does.** The domain synthesis's ledger, with biology cutting 90%
of every source but the waters and the waters at the flux the lakes' and seas' methane study traced at 301 K, against
the soils' uptake of 2.92 Tg a year for each ppm:

| The waters, with Earth's methanotrophs | Their methane | The air left to the soils | Removal to hold 1.87 ppm; air through the contactors a year | Removal to hold the levers' low pair, 0.3 ppm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nutrient-poor | 0.57 Tg a year | 1.01–1.75 ppm | none | 2.1–4.2 Tg; 0.39–0.80% |
| Mesotrophic | 7.8 Tg | 3.5–4.2 ppm | 4.7–6.9 Tg; 0.14–0.21% | 9.3–11.5 Tg; 1.8–2.2% |
| Eutrophic | 34 Tg | 12.5–13.2 ppm | 31–33 Tg; 0.94–1.01% | 36–38 Tg; 6.7–7.1% |
| New reservoirs throughout | 57 Tg | 20–21 ppm | 54–56 Tg; 1.6–1.7% | 59–61 Tg; 11.1–11.5% |

With the methanotrophs designed for sudden overturns, mesotrophic waters send 4.9 Tg a year and need 1.9–4.0 Tg of
removal at the held pair. The low pair saves 0.79–0.81 points of dimming (heat levers); its methane contactors cost
about 0.07 TW for each 1% of the air passed a year, 0.03–0.06 TW with nutrient-poor waters and 0.12–0.15 TW with
mesotrophic ones, while the air they handle grows from under 1% of the air a year to about 2% and, with eutrophic
waters, 7%. The low pair's N2O decomposition, 0.96–1.47 TW, stays its larger cost. Nutrient-poor waters let the held
pair stand with no removal at all and hold the low pair with the contactors passing under 1% of the air a year; every
richer state needs removal at either pair. The air loses its methane to the soils and, beyond them, to the contactors:
at the held pair the soils take 5.5 Tg a year from an air holding 3,290 Tg, a turnover of about 600 years; at the low
pair the contactors, passing 0.4–0.8% of the air a year, take its methane in 125–250 years.

**The deep lakes stay nutrient-poor only while people send them little dissolved phosphorus.** The lakes' anoxia study
draws the oligotrophic line at a bioavailable load of 0.158 g per m² a year with Earth's settling, 0.075 with settling
at the square root of the gravity ratio and 0.041 at the full ratio. The land's own dissolved phosphorus brings the 3.92
million km² of lakes 0.017–0.045 g. What is left under the line, shared among the people on the Moon:

| Settling | Room for people's dissolved phosphorus, a person a year, at 4 and 8 billion | Share of the design loss, 44–180 g a person, that may arrive dissolved, at 8 billion |
|---|---|---|
| Earth's | 111–139 g; 56–69 g | 31–158% |
| The square root of g/6 | 30–57 g; 15–29 g | 8–65% |
| g/6 | up to 24 g, none at the high land load; up to 12 g | none to 27% |

The plan loses 44–180 g of phosphorus a person a year (the food study and the sea phosphorus screen), against 1.76–2.4
kg in Earth's practice. Most of a field's loss leaves bound to soil, and the deltas keep it, but the vented glass
houses' bleed alone carries 0–90 g of phosphorus a person a year in nutrient solution. Kept from the deep lakes,
recovered as struvite or sent to the seas, the dissolved part stays under the line; let into the lakes at the middle
settling, it alone can carry them past it. The trophic state is a tipping point (lake anoxia): once a deep lake's floor
goes anoxic it gives back what the floor held, the methane follows the table above, and the giants, 70% of the lake
area, go anoxic at depth when mesotrophic.

**The seas get almost none of it.** The lakes hold 96% of the land's phosphorus, and the held fresh seas reach 0.27–1.1
mg/m³ of dissolved phosphorus at 1,000 years (sea phosphorus), clearer and poorer than Earth's open ocean at 70 mg/m³.
The author held the seas fresh on the condition that they are rich in life. Richer seas take phosphorus the plan does
not now send them: with the lakes' dissolved load passed straight through, 4–17 mg/m³ at 1,000 years, and at mesotrophic
levels the seas' own methane reaches 4.3 Tg a year (the waters' methane), with the area-weighted deepest tenth at 2.1
mg/L under Osborn's rule and 0.9 mg/L with Shih and Bouffard's form at the two great basins (the seas' oxygen).
Life-rich seas are a design choice with that price; nutrient-poor seas are what the plan now makes.

**The resort's dimming with the levers worth pursuing.** At 301 K the resort's own dimming is 4.5–5.6% of the hot
world's absorbed sunlight and 8.2–10.9% with the gases, the glow and the beamed power answered (the targets). The
levers' first four, the glow's set-aside answers, food made in orbit, the low gas pair and white roofs with silver
foliage, save 1.6–3.0 points together, which leaves 5.2–9.3% in all; with nutrient-poor waters the low pair's methane
half costs the contactors under 0.06 TW.

**Watering the deserts: a scale.** The resort's deserts, land under 0.5 mm of rain a day, are 4.9–6.2 million km² by the
interpolation's form (coast water), most of them poleward of 45°: all the land beyond 60° is desert, 3.5 million km²,
and 1.2–2.1 million km² lies at 45–60°, 0.2–0.6 million km² at 30–45°. A garden there takes 1.94 mm a day beyond the
desert's rain, about 0.71 million m³ a year for each km² (coast water's FAO-56 demand). Lifted from the groundwater, a
median 470–770 m down by band, with pumps at 70% and 0.1–0.3 kWh/m³ to carry it:

| Share of the desert watered | Land | Water a year (share of the rivers' 7,240 km³) | Power | Its heat | The sunlight a greener ground keeps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1% | 49,000–62,000 km² | 35–44 km³ (0.5–0.6%) | 1.6–3.9 GW | 0.00003–0.00007 points | 0.002–0.009 points |
| 5% | 0.25–0.31 million km² | 175–220 km³ (2.4–3.0%) | 8–20 GW | under 0.0004 points | 0.01–0.04 points |
| 10% | 0.49–0.62 million km² | 350–440 km³ (4.8–6.1%) | 16–39 GW | under 0.0007 points | 0.02–0.09 points |
| 25% | 1.2–1.5 million km² | 870–1,100 km³ (12–15%) | 40–99 GW | under 0.002 points | 0.05–0.22 points |

The heat is the power's, with the beams' 1.5 W for each watt delivered. The greener ground's albedo is 0.10–0.20 below
the desert's (a design guess), carried by the levers' desert kernel by latitude; the high end is the lock's kernel and
the log-linear form's deserts. The water returns as rain, so the cycle closes, but where it falls is the GCM's to say,
and the deserts' groundwater, recharged by 0.17 mm of rain a day, would be mined unless the lakes and rivers feed it.
Gardens poleward of 60° also meet the low Sun and its cold. The scale leaves the choice to the author.

## Assumptions and limits

- The methane budget is the lakes' and seas' methane study's, at its central methane share and Earth's methanotrophs
  unless named; its brackets (the share's 0.8, Shih and Bouffard's mixing) run in that study.
- The phosphorus lines are the lakes' anoxia study's, from Vollenweider's relation on the 352 m lakes' depth and
  residence; the room is shared evenly over the Moon's lakes, while people's losses gather near towns and farms.
- People's dissolved phosphorus is taken as wholly bioavailable; the land's is the sea phosphorus screen's bioavailable
  input.
- The watering scale's lift is the groundwater's median depth under the deserts, from the 30–45° band's to the whole
  deserts'; from lakes or rivers the lift is each garden's height above its water. The albedo cost carries the levers'
  kernels for the hot world's and the lock's deserts, which bracket the resort's.
- Nothing here runs a climate model: the deserts' rain under watering, the seas' life and the lakes' chemistry under
  people's loads are for the 301 K design case and its studies.
