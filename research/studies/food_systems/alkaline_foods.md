# H2: alkaline habitats and distinctive seafood chemistry

4 November 2026, revised 7 November 2026. A hypothesis and a proposed study within the [aquatic-food study](aquatic_novelty.md), on Earth literature read to the depth its source list records. The target is a desirable, distinctive food from a line that thrives and breeds in its habitat; stress metabolites alone miss it.

## Habitats before organisms

[The sea chemistry](../sea_chemistry/README.md) separates three environments that the food study keeps separate. It is a model with older thermal inputs, and its composition is a starting scenario to reconcile with the design climate. The managed route leaves the held seas no magnesium, and their chemistry is to be worked out on domain/resources with magnesium kept at 0.05–0.5 mmol/kg ([register, 19 October](../../decisions.md#the-design-climate-and-the-waters-19-october-2026)); until then a rearing medium specifies its own magnesium.

| Habitat | Defining chemistry | Role for food |
|---|---|---|
| Held common-level fresh seas | About 0.14 g/kg of dissolved salts at pH 8.3, calcium–bicarbonate water with an alkalinity of roughly 1.5 mmol/L (*computed* in the review of 6 November), under active mineral management | The reference habitat for ordinary freshwater founders; a pH near 8.3 alone gives no reason to expect shellfish-like sweetness |
| Moderately concentrated lakes, spring-fed margins and controlled finishing waters | Site-specific mixtures of bicarbonate and carbonate, sodium, calcium, magnesium and minor ions | The first H2 test space, after screening for a complete life cycle and a suitable food web. The bass study's favoured 15–20 mmol/L [A2] is about ten times the fresh seas' alkalinity, an early stage on the way to a soda lake (*computed* in the review) |
| Mature closed soda lakes | Sodium carbonate and bicarbonate at a pH near 10, with calcium below 0.01 mmol/kg; their concentration grows with age, reaching the ocean's salinity in about 100,000 years at 10 m deep (*product*; [register, 15 October](../../decisions.md#soda-lakes-15-october-2026)). They form in the closed basins of the dry belts, and drylands cover 59.5% of the land in the turned design years at the target ([register, 7 November](../../decisions.md#the-food-systems-branch-as-ideation-the-registers-pin-the-world-on-the-turned-moon-and-the-sixth-integration-7-november-2026)) | Their own local ecosystems under that row: a specialist community for comparative physiology, where any harvest is a question for the row |

Salinity measures the dissolved salts, alkalinity the capacity to neutralize acid, hardness mainly the calcium and magnesium, and pH the hydrogen-ion activity. They interact, and each needs its own control: a sodium-chloride experiment tests salinity, a high pH supplies no calcium for shells, and very alkaline water changes how a fish sheds its nitrogen. At 29.6 °C the toxic, un-ionized share of ammonia is 0.8% of ammonia nitrogen at pH 7, 13.5% at the held seas' pH 8.3 and 89% at a soda lake's pH 10 (*computed* in the review of 6 November), so at pH 10 ammonia limits fed fish before taste does.

**The ecological route:** a mildly concentrated basin; periphyton, plankton and benthic prey suited to it; an alkali-tolerant omnivore or predator; heritable tissue chemistry and texture; a regional food. Each step is established on its own. A productive algal mat can feed grazers without making their flesh desirable, and an attractive finishing treatment can remain an aquaculture technique while the lake holds no wild fishery.

## What the Earth evidence supports

| Evidence | Observed result | What it means for H2 |
|---|---|---|
| Largemouth bass, 10 weeks at 0, 3 and 9 g/L salinity [A1] | Texture and taste-active compounds changed; the 9 g/L group had more IMP, and the sweet and umami free amino acids rose | A positive comparator for chemical plasticity, from a salinity experiment at 3 and 9 g/L against the fresh seas' 0.14 g/kg |
| Largemouth bass, an 80-day alkalinity experiment [A2] | The authors report changes in muscle quality and favour 15–20 mmol/L within their tested conditions | The most direct lead; its full methods, alkalinity convention and ion recipe come before any exposure is adopted |
| Giant freshwater prawn, saline-alkaline against fresh water [A3] | More taste-active compounds and over twice the **calculated equivalent umami concentration** | Supports testing a chemical mechanism; EUC is a model index of umami, and whether people taste or prefer the difference needs a tasting |
| A separate giant freshwater prawn comparison [A4] | More IMP, fewer umami amino acids, and no significant difference in umami | The response is conditional; A3 and A4 are reconciled through chemistry, diet, stock, life stage and handling |
| Mozambique tilapia under separate salinity, alkalinity and combined stress [A5] | Gill metabolomics changed, amino acids and osmolytes among them | Supports screening this line's mechanism; muscle flavour and reproduction are measured separately |
| Magadi tilapia, *Alcolapia grahami* [A6] | Urea excretion persists after gradual dilution to nearly fresh water | Specialized nitrogen physiology, the trait that lets a fish live at pH 10; muscle urea, sweetness and odour are separate measurements |

A4 is valuable because it records the unfavourable result beside A3: one nucleotide can rise while the amino acids fall. Neither the taste of isolated compounds nor a chemical or electronic index replaces sensory evaluation of the finished food.

## Founders and comparators

These are candidates for contained evaluation, from captive stock.

| Line | Habitat and role | Breeding objective | Culinary direction and decisive limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Oreochromis niloticus* | The fresh-sea baseline and an omnivore for moderate carbonate lakes; diets supported by periphyton | Reproduction, efficient growth, little off-flavour, a firm fillet and favourable measured taste chemistry | Pale cooked fish and a possible [qualified](aquatic_novelty.md#terms) raw product; Earth work on juveniles' pH range [A7] leaves soda water untested |
| *O. mossambicus* | A chemistry-tolerant comparison and possible founder for moderate lakes. Vulnerable on the IUCN Red List, threatened in its home range by hybridization with introduced Nile tilapia [A10], so founders come from captive lines of documented purity | Performance over the whole life cycle in the target ion mixture, then muscle traits | A possible regional savoury whitefish; lines kept separate at first, since stress tolerance is a different merit from flavour [A5] |
| *Micropterus salmoides* | A contained experimental predator and the positive comparator in the literature | Whether the quality effects recur in carbonate water and the Moon's warm regimes | Firm slices, or steamed and grilled fillets; release into open waters is a separate question under the rules for what lives wild [A1, A2] |
| *Macrobrachium rosenbergii* | Managed grow-out in fresh or moderately concentrated water; a benthic omnivore with its own hatchery | Growth, moult success, survival and the muscle chemistry sought, at compatible hardness and alkalinity | Cooked ebi, grilled tails and broth; its larvae need brackish water, so the fresh seas leave its life cycle open [A8] |
| *Alcolapia grahami* | A comparator for extreme chemistry. Vulnerable on the IUCN Red List and the only fish of Lake Magadi [A11], so it enters from captive stock alone | Tolerance and nitrogen disposal first | A research organism; its ecology and conservation are reviewed before any founder proposal [A6] |

Tilapia trials use screened periphyton, phytoplankton, detrital and formulated-feed treatments of measured composition, and prawn trials benthic diets and shelters, with the sediment's oxygen demand and the nutrient losses recorded. These are trial food webs, whose coexistence the trials test. Toxin-producing blooms and exposure to geosmin and 2-MIB are kept apart from nutritional composition.

Prawns get a restrictive first screen, because a 2017 study of postlarvae reports deaths in some high-alkalinity treatments [A9]. Its abstract leaves the concentration convention and exposure conditions unclear, so it sets no numerical threshold here; it shows that carbonate-rich water can harm as well as help. A9's complete chemistry, alkalinity convention, exposure time and life stage are harmonized with the later flavour studies before live comparisons, and high hardness and high alkalinity can act differently.

## Selective breeding and the evaluation of lines

Breeding starts from several documented Earth populations or established farm families, with unselected reference stocks kept. Genomic relatedness and pedigrees separate family effects from shared rearing, and enough families are kept represented. The same families are compared in reference fresh water and in the target chemistry; a family that tastes good only while physiologically impaired is a poor candidate.

The selection index includes survival and reproduction over the full life cycle, welfare, growth and feed use, fillet yield, the texture sought and measured sensory preference. The flavour measurements are free glutamate and aspartate, glycine and alanine, IMP, AMP and GMP, lipid composition and oxidation; urea, ammonia and off-flavour compounds are measured with their direction left open. Muscle is measured apart from gill, liver, haemolymph or plasma.

Common-garden offspring and reciprocal rearing tell inherited differences from acclimation. A marker associated with a phenotype is a prediction until it holds across families and generations, and a finishing effect of the environment stays a separate product route. Organisms on the Moon are engineered, with Earth traits as design variables ([register, Life and people](../../decisions.md#life-and-people)): every line that lives wild is bred first and then given the shared signals, reporting traits and DNA watermark ([register, 6 November](../../decisions.md#the-food-systems-branchs-evaluation-wild-lines-from-earth-stock-the-eels-seawater-spawning-and-organisms-crossing-between-worlds-6-november-2026)), and engineering a tolerance or flavour trait, such as urea excretion for soda water (an idea of the review of 6 November), is a design choice these trials inform.

## A staged experiment

1. **Chemistry and life cycle.** The full methods of A2–A4 and A9 are obtained. The modelled target waters are characterized by pH, total alkalinity in charge equivalents, inorganic carbon, osmolality, conductivity, sodium, potassium, calcium, magnesium, chloride, sulphate and trace elements, with precipitation and speciation checked. Exposures come from ranges shown harmless for each life stage, so a bass treatment never transfers to prawn postlarvae. Welfare stopping rules are set in advance, and normal feeding, growth and reproduction come before any selection for flavour.
2. **The mechanisms separated.** Within feasible chemistry the trial compares reference fresh water, chloride-dominated salinity controls, carbonate-enriched water, and carbonate water with altered pH and CO2, matching osmolality and major ions where chemistry allows and recording what cannot be matched. Carbonate alkalinity, pH and CO2 are coupled, so the design is constrained. Temperature, oxygen, diet, stocking and handling stay comparable.
3. **Water separated from food.** After the direct-water screen, the supported water treatments are crossed with a standard diet and a habitat-derived diet of measured composition, so that a favourable algal diet is never credited to alkalinity. The lunar light cycle enters once the chemistry effect can be estimated, with the habitat's underwater light trace.
4. **Biological units replicated.** Independent tanks are randomized and families distributed among them; the tank is the replicate, and fish within it are subsamples. Pilot variance and a preregistered minimum effect on sensory or production traits size the confirmatory trial. Samples are matched for size, sex and maturity, lunar phase and the interval since harvest, and family-by-environment interactions are recorded.
5. **Actual food tested.** Identically cooked, blinded samples come first, with preference, texture, aftertaste, aroma and yield measured beside the chemistry. Steamed and grilled fish, and steamed and grilled prawn, are compared, since heat changes which precursor differences matter. Only material qualified for raw service enters raw sensory work, with matched freezing, thawing and storage.

**H2 succeeds** if a tolerable habitat gives a repeatable, desirable culinary difference that breeding can keep or improve at acceptable cost to reproduction, nutrition and ecology. If the effect disappears under a matched diet, the direct-water mechanism weakens and an indirect food-web mechanism gains support. The culinary proposal weakens if the changes come from stress or processing, fail the sensory test, or need a chemistry the habitat lacks. A benefit from finishing alone is still useful, and it supports a finishing product in place of a distinctive lake line.

## Culinary outcomes to carry forward

The leading product is a moderate-lake whitefish with a firm bite and a measurable savoury or sweet profile. Its first dishes are steamed fillets, lightly grilled fish and cooked sushi toppings, with qualified sashimi as a later option. The prawn route begins with cooked ebi, grilled tails and stock. Fish from extreme lakes stay research organisms until their edibility, harvest scale and processing are established.

The evidence gives no colour phenotype from alkaline water; colour and pigments are measured, and a new colour would be a trait to test. "Alkaline sweetfish" names a target flavour, and it is distinct from Earth's sweetfish, *Plecoglossus altivelis*.
