# Aquatic foods: from biomes to lineages to cuisine

4 November 2026 · branch `domain/food-systems`, opened from main
`b7eddacd674270ba8365f82a2d1bd5c3001ca883` at the author's request.

**This is the first evidence synthesis and experimental design for distinctive
Terlunan aquatic foods.** It extends the existing [food-system accounts](README.md),
whose numerical products and chosen diet blend remain the provisioning baseline.
No new fishery yield, evolved organism, breeding response or food-safety outcome
has been demonstrated here. The scientific objective is to identify combinations
of habitat, Earth ancestry and husbandry that make desirable culinary properties
more likely under the Moon's conditions.

## The author's direction

- Work from **biomes → food webs → founder species and lineages → breeding →
  harvested tissues → culinary preparations**. Treat farmed and wild populations
  separately, including their connections.
- Concentrate first on three ideas: cyclic lipid-rich foods; sweet or savory
  foods from alkaline habitats; and carbonate-associated communities yielding
  distinctive pale fish and shellfish.
- Start with Earth species, choose among existing lineages, then use intensive
  selective breeding supported by pedigrees, genomic analysis and measured
  performance. Genetic engineering and synthetic biology remain later options
  for a documented limitation. Terrestrial biological continuity is a preference;
  ordinary breeding alone does not establish ecological safety.
- Cooked seafood is a full product category. Eel/unagi, other kabayaki-style fish,
  grilled fatty fish, broths and cooked shellfish need their own objectives.
  Raw suitability is an additional qualification for selected products.
- Assume continuing biological exchange with Earth. For qualified wild fisheries,
  retain the objective of risk comparable to contemporary regulated sushi after
  appropriate processing and surveillance. Neither isolation nor freezing alone
  supplies that outcome.

These are recorded in the [decision register](aquatic_decisions.md).

## Three hypotheses and what would count as progress

The names below are working product descriptions, not taxonomic assignments or
claims that the corresponding fish already exists.

| Hypothesis | Environmental mechanism to test | Desired food property | Evidence already available | Result that would weaken it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1: lunar-cycle fatfish** | Long illumination cycles change primary-producer reserves and prey availability; some consumers store the resulting energy in edible tissue | Fine muscle fat, a favorable melting profile, predictable rich and lean harvest phases | Earth plankton show daily lipid cycling; fish differ in their fat depots and dietary response | Energy accumulates mainly outside edible fish tissue; food-web lag smooths the cycle; the actual cyclic food web produces no useful edible-muscle response |
| **H2: alkaline sweet/savory fish** | Water ions and acid-base conditions change compatible solutes, nitrogen handling and tissue metabolism | Desirable free amino acids and nucleotides, balanced sweetness and umami with useful firmness | Specific saline/alkaline rearing studies change measured flavor chemistry; effects differ between studies | Only stress, liver changes or reduced growth result; calculated umami rises without blinded sensory benefit; ordinary salinity explains the effect, weakening carbonate-specific novelty |
| **H3: carbonate-community pale fish** | Structured habitat supports particular prey and feeding strategies; lineage and diet determine flesh composition | Pale, sliceable or flaky flesh, localized fat and distinctive clean flavor; complementary shellfish products | Earth dietary trials and established freshwater aquaculture provide tractable founders; existing mineral work supports studying living carbonate communities | The habitat supplies no sustainable distinctive diet or useful culinary benefit; prey demand exceeds production; off-flavor, biodeposition or oxygen cost overwhelms the benefit |

Matched-ration or matched-diet controls distinguish mechanisms: disappearance of
a difference can support mediation through food supply or diet, while weakening
a direct photoperiod or habitat-structure effect. It is not by itself a failure
of the broader food-web hypothesis. Algal carbohydrate storage likewise weakens
the direct lipid-rich-prey route without ruling out consumer fat made from other
dietary energy. A salinity-only H2 effect can remain useful food production while
losing its carbonate-specific interpretation.

H1 has the most distinctive lunar forcing, but the chain from photons to muscle
fat has several unverified links. H2 offers a measurable chemistry experiment,
with no general rule that greater alkalinity improves taste. H3 offers a practical
way to assemble a local cuisine, with a weaker claim that the same product could
not also be produced on Earth. Novelty can lie in **the coupled ecology, reliable
harvest timing and regional food tradition**, even when every lineage descends
from terrestrial species and the biochemistry uses familiar compounds.

## Initial portfolio

These are **research priorities**, not released species or final stocking choices.
Species-level evidence and life-cycle qualifications are in the linked chapters.

| Priority track | First tractable candidates | Broader ecological comparison | First culinary comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cyclic edible fat | Jade perch (*Scortum barcoo*) and a pedigreed Nile tilapia (*Oreochromis niloticus*) reference | Freshwater clupeids such as *Limnothrissa miodon*, with their thermal and recruitment limits; plankton and prey before a predator fishery | Matched cut and handling at several local cycle phases: fatty raw candidate where qualified, fully cooked nigiri topping, plain grilled fish |
| Moderate alkaline food chemistry | Nile/Mozambique tilapia lineages; giant freshwater prawn as a separate invertebrate comparison | Soda-lake *Alcolapia* as a mechanistic comparator, with no assumed table-size or conservation feasibility | Plain steamed fish/prawn first; raw fish only after separate qualification; flavored recipes after the intrinsic taste comparison |
| Carbonate-associated fish and shellfish | Jade perch or tilapia references, freshwater prawns/crayfish in compatible water, pearl mussels as habitat/material organisms | Barramundi only with its hatchery needs addressed; locally appropriate grazers, prey and host fish chosen together | Pale fish raw candidate; skin-on roast or kabayaki-style fillet; cooked crustaceans; bivalves only if food use is independently justified |
| Eel and cooked rich-flesh track | Evaluate actual *Anguilla* with a closed hatchery life cycle, alongside freshwater catfish as a preparation comparator | Migration, saline reproduction, recruitment cost and lineage welfare | Actual eel for unagi; other species identified by name with kabayaki-style preparation |

Overlap is deliberate. A common reference lineage across experiments helps
separate a habitat effect from unrelated species differences. Marine herring,
tuna and salmon can be analytical or culinary comparators without being proposed
for the open fresh seas. Large predators are a later trophic-budget decision.

## What the branch has established

1. A coherent study can use the existing basin and chemistry work now. It must
   preserve the actual mixing brackets and distinguish the old interpolated 301 K
   forcing from the newer GCM design years and the pending reoriented climate.
2. A 354-hour interval with the Sun below the horizon is not a universal interval
   of zero underwater photosynthesis. Twilight, Earthlight, latitude, cloud and
   water attenuation belong in the exposures. Fish can continue feeding at night.
3. The alkaline opportunity needs a factorial chemistry comparison. Different prawn studies and
   conditions do not establish universal sweetening, and a
   calculated equivalent umami concentration is not a blinded tasting result.
4. Earth fish already provide variation in edible fat and response to feed.
   Choosing founders and breeding within them is a substantive route to explore
   before altering genomes. Breeding targets need fertility, health and ecological
   performance beside growth and flavor.
5. Food, pearl/nacre production, mineral sequestration and ecosystem habitat can
   share a community, but cannot all claim the same biomass or mineral removal.
   Shellfish filtration also redistributes organic matter to sediments.

The next desk-research unit is **three paired founder dossiers**: jade perch versus
tilapia for edible fat, tilapia versus prawn for moderate alkaline chemistry, and
the chosen fish plus a complete shellfish/prey community for carbonate habitats.
Actual eel receives a parallel life-cycle dossier for the cooked track. Each dossier
must be useful even if its proposed lunar advantage fails.

## Domain interfaces

- **Biosphere/ecology:** organism traits, food webs, reproduction, evolution,
  community effects and exposure to new organisms.
- **Climate/hydrosphere and illumination:** temperature, oxygen-relevant mixing,
  local underwater light, currents and extremes. Reuse their products; do not
  infer a new circulation solution from food preferences.
- **Resources/aquatic biogeochemistry:** phosphorus, essential-ion floors, sediment,
  calcium and magnesium balances. Food withdrawal, feed input and returned waste
  need one shared account.
- **Provisioning:** hatchery and grow-out systems, feed, chilling/freezing/cooking,
  water treatment, power/heat, logistics, waste return and food allocation.
- **Human food culture:** regional dishes and harvest practices are design
  possibilities following the evidence; they do not establish biochemical traits.

The author has authorized this food-system study. Its scope does not require a
new climate run or an assumed completion of the whole aquatic biogeochemistry
programme. Numerical development stays in the appropriate existing domain;
this cross-domain folder owns the synthesis and study design.
