# Founders, selective breeding and comparative trials

4 November 2026. **Study design; no new biological experiments or breeding outcomes.** This is the common testing framework for [lunar-cycle fisheries](lunar_cycle_fisheries.md), [alkaline foods](alkaline_foods.md) and [carbonate habitats and shellfish](reef_shellfish_cuisine.md). It extends the existing food-systems study without changing its numerical production assumptions.

## The development order

The author's order is **Earth species and existing lineages → husbandry and ecological assembly → intensive selective breeding informed by pedigrees and genomes → genetic engineering or synthetic biology where a remaining requirement justifies it**. Begin with organisms whose complete life cycles, culinary properties and variation are already accessible. Keep identifiable founder ancestry and reference stocks throughout development.

This gives comparative biology a usable baseline and preserves more terrestrial biological continuity than designing every function afresh. It does not establish that a selected lineage is harmless on Earth or Terluna. Selection can alter fecundity, competitive ability, dispersal and host relationships; ordinary organisms can also carry pathogens. Evaluate the phenotype and ecological exposure in both directions under continuing interworld exchange. Food quality, ecological suitability and raw-consumption qualification are separate decisions.

Earth evidence supports trying this route. A Nile tilapia study estimated additive variation in fillet fat and yield; selecting only growth did not reliably imply improvement in those flesh traits [B1]. A separate study improved genomic prediction of harvest weight and fillet yield relative to its pedigree model [B2]. These establish useful breeding tools and variation in particular Earth populations. They provide neither a transferable lunar heritability nor a promised rate of culinary improvement.

## Stage 0: admit a founder and define its destination

Record each candidate as a named species, population and hatchery line, with provenance, pedigree completeness and reference samples. A common name or species-level genome does not identify the particular founding population. Start parallel comparisons of existing strains where practical; retain an unselected reference rather than immediately pooling every source.

| Gate | Evidence required before advancing |
|---|---|
| Habitat fit | Temperature distributions and extremes, oxygen minima and exposure duration, water chemistry and seasonal/lunar variation compatible with each life stage |
| Reproduction | Eggs, larvae, juveniles and breeding adults can complete the cycle; any saline hatchery, fish host, migration or special nursery is explicitly supplied |
| Resources | Feed source, phosphorus and nitrogen budgets, oxygen demand, wastes and usable harvest fraction fit the intended biome or farm |
| Animal condition | Growth, behavior, tissue condition, normal development and reproductive performance remain acceptable; persistence alone is insufficient |
| Ecology | Predation, competition, hybridization, host dependence and escape/establishment consequences assessed for the proposed community |
| Culinary use | A defined product has measurable value: lean slices, fatty slices, grilled skin-on fillets, shellfish meat, stock or another preparation |

Read habitat envelopes from the pinned climate, [sea-oxygen](../sea_oxygen/README.md), lake and chemistry products. A basin mean or saturation concentration cannot stand in for the oxygen at an animal's occupied depth. Define species- and stage-specific limits from evidence and pilot observations; do not impose one universal oxygen threshold or infer warm-water suitability from an adult's acute survival limit.

Wild/community founders and intensive-farm lines need separate objectives. A farm line selected under steady feed and aeration may perform poorly in a lunar-cycle food web. Conversely, the traits that enable wild persistence can increase consequences of escape into an Earth catchment.

## Stage 1: separate the three mechanisms

Run small feasibility studies before the full factorial comparison. Select only tolerable treatment combinations, predefine stopping rules, and then use pilot variance and the smallest meaningful culinary difference to size the main study. No fixed tank count, family count or power is justified before those estimates exist.

| Hypothesis | Controlled contrast | Primary mechanistic endpoint | Result that weakens the proposal |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1: long light/food cycles favor useful flesh reserves | Earth-length and lunar-length lighting at matched integrated photon supply; independently vary constant versus cyclic prey/feed delivery | Phase-resolved edible-muscle lipid and lipid class, alongside liver/visceral reserves and actual feed intake | Energy accumulates mainly outside edible tissues; prey cycles disappear; or desired fat requires impaired growth/condition |
| H2: alkaline-water adaptation yields desirable taste chemistry | Independently bracket tolerable pH, alkalinity and ionic/osmotic conditions, with measured chemistry and matched diet | Free taste-active amino acids and nucleotides per mass of edible tissue, plus osmoregulatory condition | Apparent enrichment is dehydration, a salinity-only effect, stress-associated tissue change, or fails sensory confirmation |
| H3: carbonate-associated food webs create distinctive flesh | Matched physical habitat with alternative prey diets; a separate habitat-structure comparison at matched nutrition | Reproducible diet-linked flavor/texture, fatty-acid deposition and flesh yield | The proposed community produces no sustainable favorable diet or useful flesh response; a difference erased by diet matching instead supports dietary mediation |

For H1, equal photon dose tests timing while the separate **world-realistic treatment** reproduces the modeled spectrum, underwater attenuation, twilight, Earthlight and temperature. Match and report the integrated photons actually reaching producers. Equal dose does not make photosynthesis equal: intensity-dependent saturation and photoinhibition still need measurement. Fish illumination and producer illumination may require separate treatments to distinguish behavioral timing from diet supply. A 354-hour Sun-below-horizon interval is not automatically 354 hours without useful light or food.

Keep temperature and oxygen controlled in the initial mechanism comparison, then add their modeled covariance in validation. Otherwise reduced night feeding, low oxygen and warmth can be confounded with photoperiod. Measure prey abundance and composition through several complete cycles and continue until reproducibility or a continuing drift can be distinguished. Sample enough phases to estimate whether plankton, prey and fish peaks lag one another; do not assume the richest fish occur at sunset. An Earth-like internal daily rhythm may persist inside the long external cycle.

For H2, water formulations must remain chemically feasible. Measure alkalinity, conductivity/osmolality, major ions, calcium/magnesium balance, dissolved inorganic carbon, pH and nitrogenous waste. Several variables co-vary in natural soda lakes; a gradient alone cannot identify the causal one. For H3, retain food-web biomass and nutrient accounts so that feeding a premium fish does not also count the same prey as a shellfish harvest.

## Stage 2: identify plastic and inherited responses

Use common-garden rearing and split siblings from each family across treatments and independent systems. Model family, early rearing, maternal effects, sex, maturity, age/size, cohort and processing batch. Where early family separation is unavoidable, retain it in the design and analysis. A line difference can reflect maternal or common-environment effects; it is not itself a heritability estimate. Nile tilapia research has measured maternal effects and inbreeding depression alongside additive variation [B3].

For water, light or shared-feed interventions, the independently assigned tank or isolated recirculation system is the experimental unit; fish and tissue samples are subsamples. Multiple tanks connected to a single treatment-specific sump do not provide independent water-treatment replication. Randomize positions and batches, blind laboratory and sensory labels, and analyze the hierarchy explicitly [B4]. Repeated observations on a tank or fish improve temporal resolution without multiplying independent replication.

Measure destructive flesh traits on pedigree/genotyped siblings and retain breeding candidates. Calibrate any nondestructive proxy against tissue measurements in the actual species, sizes, fat distribution and treatments. Use the genomic relationship information to predict candidate merit, compare it with pedigree-only prediction, and test prediction in held-out families and later cohorts; report relatedness between training and validation sets. A predictive association does not demonstrate a causal flavor gene [B2].

Distinguish three outcomes:

- **Plastic response:** the same family changes with its environment, including reversible acclimation.
- **Genotype-by-environment interaction:** families differ in response or ranking among environments; a universal best line may not exist.
- **Inherited selection response:** descendants of selected parents outperform the contemporaneous reference under matched rearing, replicated across generations/cohorts with maternal and carryover effects considered.

Fit reaction norms or a comparable mixed model across environments and phases. Report effect sizes and uncertainty, variance components and genetic correlations. Register primary endpoints and the planned handling of multiple secondary endpoints. A common-garden generation helps reduce carryover but cannot by itself exclude persistent parental effects.

## Stage 3: select a culinary animal that remains a functioning animal

Use a multi-trait objective whose constraints precede its flavor rewards. Track growth, feed conversion, edible yield, fertility, survival, morphology, locomotion, behavior and clinical condition alongside flesh traits. Pursue desired fat distribution rather than maximal total fat. Measure covariance with oxidation, storage losses and off-flavor retention; a rich fillet that deteriorates quickly may be a poor product. Published divergent selection in rainbow trout changed flesh characteristics [B5]; trout is a mechanistic comparator, not evidence that it fits Terluna's warm seas.

Maintain balanced family contributions, pedigree and genomic relatedness records, and separate reference populations. Set allowable inbreeding change and effective population targets from a species-specific demographic/genetic plan. Estimate realized inbreeding and diversity across generations; large census numbers do not guarantee a large effective population. Avoid silently replacing diversity with a handful of highly ranked breeders.

The later engineering gate requires a named unmet function, evidence that founder/line choice and breeding have reached a relevant limitation, a proportionate comparison with ecological or husbandry alternatives, and a separate phenotype/environment assessment. Genomic analysis is useful from the beginning and does not require genome modification.

## Stage 4: connect the measured flesh to preparations

Standardize capture stress, fasting history where applicable, anatomical cut, time since death, rigor state, storage, freezing/thawing and serving temperature. Match age and size by design or analysis. Otherwise postharvest variation can masquerade as an ecological or genetic culinary effect.

| Product track | Measurements and blinded evaluations |
|---|---|
| Raw or lightly treated candidate | Lipid amount/distribution and melting behavior; fatty acids; free glutamate, glycine/alanine and IMP-related profile; shear/compression, connective tissue, drip loss, aroma and mouth-coating; only qualified samples reach a raw tasting panel |
| Fully cooked white or fatty fish | Cooking yield, moisture retention, flaking, firmness, rendered fat and aroma; test both plain cooked flesh and a defined culinary preparation |
| Unagi and kabayaki-style route | For actual eel, retain the species and life-cycle requirements; for another fish, label it accurately. Test skin integrity, bone burden, fillet cohesion, tenderness after steam/grill treatment, fat retention and glaze compatibility |
| Shellfish and mixed harvest | Edible fraction, texture, sweetness/umami and cooking yield; species-specific harvest-area hazards and processing qualification |

Instrumental concentrations and calculated equivalent umami are screening endpoints. Taste also depends on the food matrix, interactions, aroma and preparation. Use trained descriptive panels and separate preference panels with coded samples and predefined comparisons; analytical superiority does not automatically imply preference. Report units on both edible wet-mass and relevant dry-mass bases when water content differs.

Rapid freezing can protect quality and contribute to parasite control, but its name is not a validated process. Raw qualification must specify product geometry and target hazards, with verified treatment and cold-chain performance. FDA guidance separates parasites, harvest-area pathogens, natural toxins and other hazards; cooking qualification likewise requires appropriate source controls [B6]. Fully cooked products are first-class outcomes. A lineage can succeed here while remaining unsuitable for raw service.

## What Earth trials can establish

Earth facilities can test selected chemistry, thermal histories, light cycles, diets, sensory traits and inherited responses. They cannot reproduce a whole lunar sea's gravity, circulation or ecological history. A pressure vessel can match local pressure while leaving gravity unchanged. Keep low-gravity development, buoyancy behavior, reproduction and community performance as later validation requirements; do not claim lunar adaptation from photoperiod trials alone.

Archive raw observations, exclusions, family IDs, treatment allocation, chemical measurements, processing records, sensory codes and analysis versions. New outputs must identify their evidence state and inputs. The next deliverable is a preregistered pilot for a short founder list, followed by an adequately replicated experiment; there are no trial results or demonstrated lunar food organisms in this note.
