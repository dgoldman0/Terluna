# Founders, selective breeding and comparative trials

4 November 2026, revised 7 November 2026. The testing framework shared by [lunar-cycle fisheries](lunar_cycle_fisheries.md) (H1), [alkaline foods](alkaline_foods.md) (H2) and [carbonate shores and shellfish](reef_shellfish_cuisine.md) (H3): the gates a founder passes, the trials that separate the three mechanisms under the Moon's cycle, and selection that keeps a culinary animal a healthy one. It sits within the [aquatic-food study](aquatic_novelty.md) and leaves the production accounts' numbers as they are.

## The development order

Organisms on the Moon are engineered, with Earth species' traits as reference points and design variables ([register, Life and people](../../decisions.md#life-and-people)). For lines bred from Earth stock the order is set for every line that lives wild: selective breeding first, then a minimal engineering step that gives the line the shared signals, reporting traits and DNA watermark ([register, 6 November](../../decisions.md#the-food-systems-branchs-evaluation-wild-lines-from-earth-stock-the-eels-seawater-spawning-and-organisms-crossing-between-worlds-6-november-2026)). The study begins with organisms whose complete life cycles, culinary properties and genetic variation are already accessible, keeps each founder's ancestry identifiable and holds reference stocks throughout. Engineering a trait beyond the minimal step, a tolerance or a flavour, is a design choice that the trials inform by measuring what breeding achieves and where it stops.

Earth evidence supports starting from breeding. In one Nile tilapia population, fillet fat and fillet yield carry additive genetic variation, and selection on growth alone did not reliably improve them [B1]; in another, genomic prediction of harvest weight and fillet yield improved on prediction from pedigree [B2]. These give tools and variation in particular Earth populations, and heritabilities and rates of gain on the Moon come from the Moon's own trials.

Selection can change fecundity, competitiveness, dispersal and relations with hosts, and ordinary organisms carry pathogens. Each line's phenotype and exposure are therefore assessed in both directions, on the Moon and on Earth, since organisms will cross between the worlds and the design plans for the crossing (register, 6 November). Food quality, ecological fit and raw qualification are separate judgements.

## Stage 0: admitting a founder

Each candidate is recorded as a named species, population and hatchery line, with its provenance, the completeness of its pedigree and reference samples; a common name or a species-level genome leaves the founding population unidentified. Founders come from captive-bred stock, and threatened species, the Japanese eel, Mozambique tilapia and *Alcolapia grahami* among the candidates, enter only that way. Existing strains are compared in parallel where practical, with an unselected reference kept apart.

| Gate | Evidence required before advancing |
|---|---|
| Habitat fit | Temperature and its extremes through the 708.7-hour cycle, the oxygen minima and their duration, and the water chemistry, each fitting every life stage. In the Moon's waters that means the seas near 29.6 °C at the surface and 25–30 °C at depth, and no fresh water below about 15 °C ([habitats](aquatic_biomes.md)) |
| Reproduction | Eggs, larvae, juveniles and breeding adults complete the cycle; a saline nursery, host fish, migration or special nursery is supplied explicitly, such as a held seawater lake for fish that spawn in salt water |
| Resources | Feed, phosphorus and nitrogen, oxygen demand, wastes and the usable harvest fit the habitat or farm; fed lines grow in closed systems ([aquatic foods](aquatic_novelty.md#fish-in-the-moons-diet)) |
| Animal condition | Growth, behaviour, tissue condition, normal development and reproduction stay acceptable, beyond mere persistence |
| Ecology | Predation, competition, hybridization, dependence on hosts and the consequences of escape and establishment, assessed for the community proposed |
| The rules for what lives wild | For a line that could live wild: climate fit with no advantage everywhere, harmless at the Moon's scale, N2O and methane within the air's sinks, phosphorus returned, the shared signals, reporting traits and watermark, and safety robust to evolution ([register, 12 October](../../decisions.md#machines-in-the-living-world-12-october-2026)) |
| Culinary use | A defined product of measurable value: lean slices, fatty slices, grilled skin-on fillets, shellfish meat, stock or another preparation |

Habitat envelopes come from the products for the climate, [the seas' oxygen](../sea_oxygen/README.md), the lakes, the cold waters and the chemistry. The oxygen at the depth an animal occupies decides, and each species and stage gets limits from evidence and pilots; an adult's acute survival limit is a different quantity from its suitability for warm water.

Wild lines and farm lines have separate objectives. A farm line selected under steady feed and aeration may do poorly in a lunar-cycle food web, and the traits that let a line persist wild raise the stakes of its escape, on the Moon or into an Earth catchment.

## Stage 1: separating the three mechanisms

Small feasibility studies come before the full factorial comparison. Only tolerable combinations of treatments are kept, stopping rules are set in advance, and pilot variance and the smallest meaningful culinary difference then size the main study.

| Hypothesis | Controlled contrast | Primary mechanistic endpoint | Result that weakens it |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1: long cycles of light and food favour useful reserves in the flesh | Earth-length against lunar-length lighting at matched integrated photons; constant against cyclic delivery of prey or feed, varied independently | Edible-muscle lipid and lipid class through the phases, beside liver and visceral reserves and the feed actually eaten | Energy gathers outside the edible tissue; the prey cycles vanish; the fat sought requires impaired growth or condition |
| H2: adaptation to alkaline water gives desirable taste chemistry | Tolerable pH, alkalinity and ionic and osmotic conditions bracketed independently, with measured chemistry and a matched diet | Free taste-active amino acids and nucleotides per mass of edible tissue, with osmoregulatory condition | The apparent enrichment is dehydration, a salinity-only effect or a stress change in the tissue, or fails the sensory test |
| H3: carbonate-associated food webs make distinctive flesh | Matched physical habitat with alternative prey diets; a separate comparison of habitat structure at matched nutrition | Repeatable diet-linked flavour and texture, fatty-acid deposition and flesh yield | The community gives no sustainable favourable diet or useful flesh response; a difference erased by matching the diet points to dietary mediation |

For H1, an equal-photon treatment tests timing, and a separate world-realistic treatment reproduces the site's trace from the underwater light study: its spectrum, attenuation, twilight and night, which keeps the water just below the surface under 1 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ for 123–255 hours of each lunar day at latitudes 0–60° ([lunar-cycle fisheries](lunar_cycle_fisheries.md)). The photons actually reaching the producers are matched and reported, and saturation and photoinhibition are measured, since an equal dose need not give equal photosynthesis. Fish and producers may need separate lighting, to tell behavioural timing from the supply of diet.

Temperature and oxygen are held steady in the first comparison and given their modelled covariance in validation, so that reduced feeding at night, low oxygen and warmth are never mistaken for photoperiod. Prey abundance and composition are followed through several complete cycles, each 708.7 hours, until the results repeat or a drift shows, and enough phases are sampled to see whether plankton, prey and fish peak in turn. A daily internal rhythm may persist inside the long cycle.

For H2, the water recipes stay chemically feasible, with alkalinity, conductivity and osmolality, the major ions, the balance of calcium and magnesium, dissolved inorganic carbon, pH and nitrogenous waste measured; in natural soda lakes several of these co-vary, so a gradient alone cannot name the cause. For H3, the food web's biomass and nutrient accounts are kept, so that the prey fed to a premium fish is never counted again as a shellfish harvest.

## Stage 2: plastic and inherited responses

Siblings from each family are split across treatments and independent systems in common-garden rearing, and the analysis models family, early rearing, maternal effects, sex, maturity, age and size, cohort and processing batch. A difference between lines can come from maternal or shared-environment effects, and Nile tilapia work has measured maternal effects and inbreeding depression beside additive variation [B3].

For treatments of water, light or shared feed, the independently assigned tank or isolated recirculating system is the experimental unit, and fish and tissue samples are subsamples; tanks on one treatment's shared sump are one replicate [B4]. Positions and batches are randomized, laboratory and sensory labels blinded, and repeated observations add temporal resolution while the replication stays the tanks'. Destructive flesh traits are measured on genotyped siblings of the breeding candidates, any non-destructive proxy is calibrated against tissue in the species and treatments concerned, and genomic prediction is tested in held-out families and later cohorts against prediction from pedigree [B2].

The trials tell three outcomes apart:

- **Plastic response:** the same family changes with its environment, reversible acclimation included.
- **Genotype-by-environment interaction:** families differ in response or rank among environments, and a single best line may not exist.
- **Inherited response to selection:** descendants of selected parents outperform the contemporary reference under matched rearing, across generations or cohorts, with maternal and carry-over effects considered.

Reaction norms or a comparable mixed model are fitted across environments and phases, reporting effect sizes with their uncertainty, variance components and genetic correlations, with the primary endpoints registered and the handling of secondary endpoints planned.

## Stage 3: a culinary animal that stays a functioning animal

The breeding objective puts its constraints before its rewards for flavour. Growth, feed conversion, edible yield, fertility, survival, form, locomotion, behaviour and clinical condition are tracked beside the flesh traits. The target is a desired distribution of fat, measured together with its covariance with oxidation, storage losses and retained off-flavours, since a rich fillet that spoils quickly is a poor product. Divergent selection in rainbow trout changed its flesh [B5], a comparator for mechanism from a cold-water fish.

Family contributions are balanced, pedigree and genomic relatedness recorded, and separate reference populations kept. The allowed change in inbreeding and the target effective population size come from a demographic and genetic plan for each species, with realized inbreeding and diversity measured each generation, since a large census leaves the effective population to be shown. A handful of top-ranked breeders never silently replaces the line's diversity.

## Stage 4: from measured flesh to preparations

Capture stress, any fasting, the anatomical cut, the time since death, rigor, storage, freezing and thawing and the serving temperature are standardized, with age and size matched by design or analysis, so that variation after harvest is never read as an ecological or genetic effect on the food.

| Product track | Measurements and blinded evaluations |
|---|---|
| Raw or lightly treated candidate | Amount, distribution and melting of lipid; fatty acids; free glutamate, glycine and alanine, and the IMP-related profile; shear and compression, connective tissue, drip loss, aroma and mouth-coating; only [qualified](aquatic_novelty.md#terms) samples reach a raw tasting panel |
| Fully cooked white or fatty fish | Cooking yield, moisture retention, flaking, firmness, rendered fat and aroma, for plain cooked flesh and a defined dish |
| Unagi and kabayaki-style fish | For eel, the species and its life-cycle requirements; for another fish, an accurate label. Skin integrity, bone burden, fillet cohesion, tenderness after steaming and grilling, fat retention and fit with the glaze |
| Shellfish and mixed harvests | Edible fraction, texture, sweetness and umami, cooking yield; each species' harvest-area hazards and processing qualification |

Instrumental concentrations and calculated equivalent umami are screening endpoints, since taste also depends on the food matrix, interactions, aroma and preparation. Trained descriptive panels and separate preference panels use coded samples and comparisons set in advance, and units are reported on edible wet mass and, where water content differs, on dry mass. Qualification for raw service names the product's geometry and target hazards with a verified treatment and cold chain, and fully cooked products are first-class outcomes ([culinary evaluation](culinary_evaluation.md)) [B6].

## What Earth trials can establish

Earth facilities can test chemistry, thermal histories, light cycles, diets, sensory traits and inherited responses. A lunar sea's gravity, circulation and ecological history lie beyond them. A pressure vessel matches pressure and leaves gravity as it is, and at a sixth of Earth's gravity the same pressure needs six times the depth: the eel's spawning depth of 150–200 m on Earth corresponds to 0.9–1.2 km of water on the Moon (register, 6 November). Development in low gravity, buoyancy behaviour, reproduction and community performance are therefore validated on the Moon, and photoperiod trials alone show no lunar adaptation.

Raw observations, exclusions, family identities, treatment allocation, chemistry, processing records, sensory codes and analysis versions are archived, and each output names its evidence state and inputs. The next deliverable is a preregistered pilot for a short list of founders, followed by an adequately replicated experiment.
