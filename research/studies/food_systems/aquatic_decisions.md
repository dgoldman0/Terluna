# Food systems: from biomes to cuisine, 4 November 2026

This scoped register records the author’s food-system directions. The existing
[project register](../../decisions.md) remains the byte-pinned input to the historical
resource accounts; this addition introduces no change to their numerical choices.

| Decision | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Open `domain/food-systems` from current main and develop a detailed study from biomes through species to culinary preparations. Concentrate first on lunar-cycle fatty foods, alkaline-habitat taste chemistry and carbonate-community foods. | Author, 4 November 2026, current food-systems conversation | Branch opened from `b7eddacd674270ba8365f82a2d1bd5c3001ca883`; initial synthesis and trial design in [aquatic foods](aquatic_novelty.md), extending the existing food accounts. Biological experiments and new coupled simulations have not been performed. |
| Select Earth species and existing lineages first; prioritize intensive selective breeding informed by genetics and lineage evaluation. Genetic engineering and synthetic biology remain available later where needed. | Author, same request | Adopted research order. Preserve ancestry and reference lines; terrestrial continuity is a preference, not proof of safety after contamination or escape. |
| Seafood need not all be raw-safe. Develop cooked products, including actual eel/unagi and accurately identified kabayaki-style alternatives, alongside the raw-fish and shellfish possibilities. | Author, same request and preceding fisheries discussion | Separate culinary targets and product qualification. The earlier objective of comparable sushi safety for qualified wild catch remains a target; no lineage or fishery is certified by this study. |
| Treat Earth and Terluna as biologically connected worlds, with recurring introductions over time. | Author, preceding fisheries discussion supplied with this request | Included in founder, food-web, surveillance and exchange assessments; permanent isolation is not assumed. |
