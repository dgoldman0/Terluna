# Verification of the freight architecture comparison

The comparison preserves the original industrial result and all inherited settlement products. Its new cases are conditional engineering estimates. Numerical closure and accounting checks establish how those estimates follow from their inputs; they do not qualify a propulsion system, trajectory, source reserve or supplier price.

On 12 November 2026, `python -m pytest -q research/studies/initial_industrial_system/test_transport.py` passed **29 tests**. They reproduce the committed product and original reference, reject stale input identities, recover both chemical burns from independent mass-ratio checks, close handling losses and initial space-supplied inventories, reconcile Earth-launched fuel and hardware, balance source water and retained material, verify replacement imports and spare capacity, retain constructed vehicle mass during sustaining operation, account for electric power, heat and drive renewal, and check localization's additional process requirements. An initial zero-loss test exposed floating-point cancellation; the loss expression was rewritten as the sum of its physical loss terms, and the controls passed afterward.

Substantive reading compared the report with the code and result tables, including the distinction between scenario cadence and minimum requirement, the retained construction fleet, the incomplete schedule design, the selected electrical-efficiency limits and the source-preservation conditions. The comparison figure was inspected as a rendered image. Its values come from the numerical product; it depicts calculated cases and contains no observational validation.

The supplemental source reading covered the water-Hall paper's abstract, performance definitions, discussion and conclusion, plus the cryogenic-storage paper's abstract, system description and conclusion. The institutional water-Hall PDF resolves the prior publisher-access problem for this comparison. The NASA electric-drive lifetime and trajectory records received the limited coverage listed in `transport_sources.json`; no full literature review is claimed. Source bytes and hashes are preserved on the research drive.

`make check` selected the industrial and transport controls plus three check-selector controls: **48 tests passed**, together with changed-file structural checks and the layer check. The dependency-consistency helper found nothing stale and rebuilt or rebound no products. These results are recorded in `transport_checks.json`. Full-repository testing, empirical validation, an integrated trajectory, detailed mission dispatch, environmental clearance and a qualified commodity reserve were not performed.
