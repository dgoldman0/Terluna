# Verification of the revenue and resource comparison

The report is a conditional engineering and external-cash account, with speculative markets and prices. Numerical tests verify its calculations and accounting boundaries; they do not establish customer demand, a sustainable business, qualified hardware or a permitted source reserve.

On 12 November 2026, the focused command `python -m pytest -q research/studies/initial_industrial_system/test_revenue.py` passed **28 tests**. They reproduce the recorded product, reject input hash mismatches, independently recover both rocket burns with return goods, charge fuel-handling losses, enforce tank and rated-capacity limits, recover the original flows with commerce absent, reconcile source material, distinguish capsule shields from durable bus renewal and relaunch, count added guest food and commissioning stocks, close computing power and heat, exclude loss-making goods and computing portfolios, reconcile external cash and replacement purchases, and keep USD balances unchanged when U is rescaled. Synthetic catalogue controls check missing diameters, volume and density scaling, orbital filters and named-target exclusions. The installed exact JPL snapshot also reproduces the inventory product; this was not a skipped test.

The report received a substantive reading against its numerical product and inherited study boundaries. Its rendered comparison image was inspected at full size. This review checked the distinction between 2041 price units and the later occupation milestone, original and alternative transport cases, whole-system external cash and corporate profit, a provision required and a provision actually funded, proposed U normalization and an adopted exchange rate, and catalogue volumes versus permitted reserves.
