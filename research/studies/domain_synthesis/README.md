# The three domains together

The resources, ecology and provisioning branches merged on 12 October 2026, and four refreshes of main's studies
followed on the merged tree ([integration record](../../integration/domains.md)). This study fits the branches
together at the author's choices: distribution C′ (8 billion on the Moon, 4 billion on the ground and 4 billion
aloft, the land 30% wild and another 20% hybrid), fed by the food blend (power-heavy 40%, power-made 50%, fully
lamp-lit 10% of the diet), with heritage meat from the homesteads. Its [ledgers](ledgers.py)
([results/ledgers.json](results/ledgers.json)) recombine the committed products in five places where the domains
meet: heat, land, methane and N2O, phosphorus, and the sky's layers. They are arithmetic on screen products and take
under a second; every figure keeps its source's evidence state. The distributions study placed C′ with the
power-heavy mix, so here the blend's food takes its place in the totals; placing the blend's fields band by band is
that study's next run (next work, item 2).

## What each branch brought

- **Resources:** where every material comes from and how its cycle closes: the imports from the Solar-System-wide
  operation, lunar rock, the inventories and flows, nitrogen extraction, the industry's kilns and weathering, and
  phosphorus from the seas, with the lake deltas dredged as the people's distribution system beside a natural cycle.
- **Ecology:** the living sky and the land: the sky's bands and the people's share of them, the aerophytes and sky
  reefs with their guards, symbioses and living sails, the sky's food web and phosphorus, the taxonomic system (on
  hold), living light, and the machines in the living world with the starting constraints on what may live wild.
- **Provisioning:** people on the Moon: the heat and computing ledgers, the inhabited volume and the drifting towns,
  ways of living and daily life, eight food systems in seven mixes and the author's blend, cities on the land and in
  the lava tubes, the distributions of 25–30 billion with the author's C′, the wild share and the megaparks, and the
  network.

The wet Moon's own waters and soils make most of both gases: under the stack the air holds no OH, so methane leaves
only into soils, and N2O has no sink at all (ecology, findings 6 and 7). People add 7–30% of the methane at central
values and 12–23% of the N2O. Holding Earth's present levels would take 58–79 Tg of methane a year at central
values and all of the N2O. Separation itself takes little power; the burden is the air: a removal from open air
would have to pass 1.8–2.4% of the whole atmosphere through its contactors each year for methane at full capture,
and 0.11–0.16% for N2O. That is why the author's order puts biology first, with complete denitrification in the
soils and methane oxidizers in the lakes and wetlands, treatment where the gases are concentrated next, and removal
from open air last. How far each gas may rise is set by its warming in the deep column, still uncomputed (next work,
item 1); the levels in these tables are Earth's, for reference.

## What it means for the whole

The author's choices fit together on the land. C′ with the blend leaves 66–75% of the dry land wild beside the
megaparks' fifth, more than the half the author prefers, at every corner of the totals. Heat is the cost of that
land, and it is the largest tension between the domains: the blend's power-made and lamp-lit food make food the
Moon's largest human heat, and at the high-cost end people and the ring fleet together would need several percent of
the sunlight taken away, which the plants then lose. Power from the array halves people's share of it.

The gases are first an ecological design problem. The wild's lakes, wetlands and soils make most of the methane and
N2O, and a wet Moon under a shield with no ultraviolet window keeps both for centuries or longer, so the biomes'
own budgets (oxidizers in the waters, denitrification that ends in N2) decide the air before any machine does. The
warming of both gases in the deep column sets how much of that work is needed.

Phosphorus closes in steady state: people's losses go to the lakes and come back by dredging, 8–51% of what the lakes
receive, while the natural cycle keeps its carriers and the lakes bury the rest. The sky is coherent from the ground
to the flight band, with two places of contact, the towns among the reefs and the network among the tallest storms.
