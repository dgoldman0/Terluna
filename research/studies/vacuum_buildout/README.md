# Withdrawn proportional build-out calculation

The year-200 calculations introduced in commits `71cbcbe3` and `6f3ab3e6` are withdrawn from the current assessment. They replicated founding industrial assets and replacement obligations with population, retained those assets after construction tapered, and limited external sales without dispatching production accordingly. Their funding requirements and mining totals therefore describe that proportional configuration, not the planned industrial expansion.

The historical code, results, figures and numerical verification remain in those commits. Numerical consistency did not establish that the configuration represented a maturing industrial economy. The replacement calculation sizes production, power, transport and replacement from actual demand in provisioning, evaluates finite source requirements in resources, and passes their versioned products to the humanities returns account.

The current [staged industrial returns study](../enclosed_returns/README.md) consumes the replacement provisioning and resources products.
