# Deprecated synthetic training data

These records were produced for earlier Terluna worldbuilding and model-training experiments. They include fictional histories, synthetic conversations, provisional scenarios and later corrections.

The active research and paper ensemble supersede that workflow. The records are retained for provenance and concept recovery; they are excluded from scientific evidence, parameter calibration and feasibility verification. Their presence does not establish that any described future event or environment exists.

The [historical training log](HISTORICAL_LOG.md) preserves the former main README's experiment account. Existing dataset bytes remain unchanged. New research belongs in the subject folders and [research index](../../research/README.md).
