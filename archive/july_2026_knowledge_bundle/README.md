Read it for its ideas: fire at 0.16 g, artificial gravity as a public utility,
civil time across the 29.53-day cycle, a staged commissioning order with
pressure plateaus and oxygen added last, the transition away from low lunar
orbit, and the terminator as a moving weather front. These are carried as open
questions in [research/status.json](../../research/status.json).
