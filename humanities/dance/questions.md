# Open questions for the author

Choices the dance work has made provisionally and would like the author's word on, in the order they matter.

1. **The first dancer.** The body is a 1.64 m, 55 kg trained woman, sized to the CC0 female base mesh the renderer
   skins. A second body (the male base mesh, or other statures and builds) comes with partnering. Is a female soloist
   the right first dancer?
2. **The first garments.** A full circle skirt and a pair of water sleeves, ordinary construction, after the earlier
   direction of large flowing outfits without attachments. Other garments to try first (a long dress, a wide-sleeved
   robe, a cape)?
3. **The venue.** Indoor, still air at the sea-level datum (1.40 kg/m^3), on a vinyl dance floor with a friction
   coefficient of 0.5. Lunar dance halls could carry floors of higher grip or banked rings for travel at speed; the
   habitation note's halls for moving are 6 m high. Should the dance assume Earth-like floors, or design its own
   stage?
4. **Music.** Lunar flights set slow pulses (a 2.7 s flight is three beats at 66 a minute) under quick articulation.
   Should phrases be timed to music, and is there music to work with?
5. **Partnering and groups.** Lifts, tosses and catches are among the most distinctly lunar movements; they need a
   two-body model. When should that come, against deepening the solo vocabulary?
6. **Wings and apparatus.** The sky-ship world has personal wings, and the six families include suspended and
   aerodynamic dance. They stay outside the flowing solo dance; should one of them follow it?
7. **Names.** The lunar units carry working names (orbit run, skim, flight phrase, slow salto, unfolding turn, carve,
   canopy, long fall, long penché). Rename any?
