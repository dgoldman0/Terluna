## Argument and independence

### Physical environment

The eight-paragraph sequence develops planetary energy coupling, vertical atmospheric structure, escape/protection, moving solar forcing, water geography, evolving surface feedbacks, disturbance recovery, and the persistence of varied regional environments. The gravity-to-escape transition connects the extended atmosphere's opportunities to its upper boundary. Protection then feeds back into the sunlight driving circulation; circulation and water placement precede the ecological/surface feedback discussion. Moving the surface-feedback passage ahead of the water-geography passage would remove the described reservoirs and exchanges on which it depends. The conclusion follows a changing planetary surface instead of returning to the root's shoreline. A claim that regional differences necessarily intensify was narrowed to continued regional change, since the sign of the feedback requires modelling.

### Biosphere

The sequence develops reproduction and habitat modification, founder viability, whole-cycle energy, soil formation, possible living architecture, diversity of resource-use strategies, expansion/connectivity, and persistence under succession and evolution. The Apollo experiment enters substrate preparation, not as proof of field ecology. The partial-gravity result was tightened to soleus-muscle mass rather than general muscle preservation. Mechanical possibilities are explicitly followed by the constraints on viable structures and ecological relationships. Connectivity comes after communities' resource accounts because those accounts identify what connections must carry. The final paragraph extends the argument to descendants and maintained elemental availability; the illustrative forest remains one of several candidate environments.

### Human life

The sequence develops choices in space/time, practical vertical access, settlement distribution and ecological land use, domestic scheduling, collective occasions, work/arts/culture, shared environmental decisions, and later revision by residents. The land-use paragraph follows vertical access because the latter introduces additional occupancy and impact locations. Festivals follow scheduling, making collective events one use of a larger temporal design. Arts, craft, and work widen the social question beyond transport and gatherings. The conclusion concerns inhabitants' continuing ability to alter settlements and purposes. The earlier root-like departure/return and kitchen/lake framing were removed.

### Engineering

The sequence develops industrial self-expansion, the throughput scale, resource/process integration, geographically distributed power and heat, source-to-arrival transport, protection lifecycle requirements, commissioning within a changing atmosphere, renewal, and the productive capabilities left after construction. The opening was revised during review to replace the elementary factory example with a direct account of the factories as both suppliers and construction targets. The unsupported claim that co-production would be the 'most efficient' arrangement was replaced with the narrower mechanism of reducing separate handling and manufacturing demands. The conclusion follows the initial growth-to-service question. No cargo form, power source, shield geometry, growth rate or source body is selected as already adequate.

## Cross-paper and prose checks

The complete four bodies were reread continuously in silence after the changes above. The review examined their sentence/paragraph dependencies, paragraph ordering, explanatory economy, natural agency, technical scope and conclusions. The following separate lenses were applied to the same complete texts:

1. Equal-weight declaratives: linked process or design consequences were retained; the seeds still use concise enumerations where they identify coupled variables rather than stand in for the argument.
2. Sentence/paragraph inheritance: the transition chains are recorded above.
3. Reorderability: tested the meaningful paragraph transitions identified above; the different companions do not share an imposed common narrative itinerary.
4. Verbs and exact action: the physical conclusion's abstract 'design becomes a world' formulation was replaced with redistribution of heat and sediment, environmental ranges, and changing terrain.
5. Human/living presence: organisms reproduce and alter substrates; inhabitants make, use and revise settlements; engineering describes actual process and production relations. No invented historical people or testimony were introduced.
6. Transitions: neighboring topics follow the needed material, energetic, temporal or social dependency.
7. Affirmative development: the seed bodies were checked for excluded phrases and defensive contrasts. Biological and technical conditions remain attached to their claims.
8. Redundancy/audience: removed the generic experimental 'lesson' following the mouse result; retained the empirical result itself and the life-cycle question it informs.
9. Cadence: continuous silent review only. Audible review was not performed or credited.
10. Opening/conclusion arc: reviewed the pair for each seed. Abstracts are not part of this seed delivery, so the full manuscript abstract/opening/conclusion check remains deferred.

These are working editorial judgments. They neither grant final release nor claim independent review. Reading source abstracts and selected sections is not full-document admission. No numerical environmental model, biological experiment, engineering demonstrator, or sociological investigation was executed.
