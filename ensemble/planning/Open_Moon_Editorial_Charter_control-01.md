## 1. Authority and the agreed five-paper structure

The latest author instructions govern. The ensemble has one dream/root paper and four independently readable companions. Its center is a living, inhabited, open Moon. Engineering, biological viability, ecology, environmental science, and human life receive substantive treatment through distinct arguments.

- Root: **Constructing and Sustaining an Open Moon**. A scientifically constrained, evocative vision that gives the reader a place to care about and relates it to a credible research and construction pathway.
- Physical companion: **The Physical Environment of an Open Moon**.
- Biosphere companion: **Establishing and Sustaining a Lunar Biosphere**. Owns biological viability, complete life cycles, general habitability requirements, ecology, biogeochemistry, and long-term persistence, including human biological constraints where relevant.
- Human companion: **Living on an Open Moon**. Owns daily life, use of built biomes, architecture, settlement, culture, long-light-cycle routines and festivals, aerial space, mobility, social possibilities, and stewardship. Health findings enter through consequences for living and design.
- Engineering companion: **Engineering and Industry for Lunar Terraforming**.

The earlier split between a biological/human-viability paper and an ecosystem paper is superseded. The eight-paper engineering-heavy plan and sequential LUN identifiers are superseded. Expanding beyond five papers requires the author's approval.

Working titles and section titles can evolve. Subject ownership, paper count, root purpose, identifiers, and this charter change through an explicit recorded decision.

## 3. Ensemble identity and standalone form

Paper IDs use UUIDv4 prefixes: 12 hexadecimal digits displayed as `8-4`. Retain the full UUID in metadata, keep each short ID across revisions, check the available register for collisions, and never recycle an ID. IDs identify papers; revision numbers identify versions. `paper_manifest.json` records the newly assigned identities and the scope of the collision check.

Every paper has its own title, abstract, introduction, developed argument, conclusion, bibliography, author metadata, linked ORCID, and essential assumptions. A reader may enter the ensemble through any paper. A compact **Ensemble Context** passage follows the introduction, using the established terms **Paper ID**, **root paper**, and **companion papers**. It explains the paper's role and relationships without becoming a roadmap for every subsequent section.

Cross-citations identify exact paper versions or release snapshots. Essential premises are stated where used. Repetition is permitted where it preserves independent readability; duplicated derivations and copied conclusions require a substantive reason. Companions may cite a shared model package, but that package's provenance and limitations remain explicit. Circular citation cannot turn a hypothesis into evidence.

The root stands as a vision/synthesis argument. Its emotional aim is attachment to the possible world; its scholarly aim is coherence among the environments, life, practices, and enabling capabilities it brings together. ## 4. Gate G1 — Argument and reorderability

**Requirement.** Each substantive prose paragraph develops a question, sequence, mechanism, comparison, consequence, or tension. Its governing relationship determines sentence order and creates a reason for the next paragraph. Sections develop a question through linked paragraphs.

**Test.** Reduce the paragraph to its assertions and explain why their order matters. Swap middle assertions or move the last assertion earlier. Trace what inferential, temporal, experiential, or evidentiary work is lost. Purposeful catalogues, parameter tables, and method inventories can use explicit list/table form.

**Blocking failures.** Arbitrarily ordered feature tours, definition series, disconnected evaluation items, or chains joined only by a shared subject. Added connectives, longer sentences, and subordinate clauses that leave the underlying inventory unchanged do not repair the argument.

**Evidence.** Paragraph location, governing relation, meaningful swap/deletion finding, and a before/after example where a passage failed. Record intentionally catalogic passages separately.

## 5. Gate G2 — Audience, specificity, and explanatory economy

**Requirement.** Calibrate to each paper's intended scholarly audience. The root addresses scientifically literate cross-disciplinary readers; the companions address their relevant specialists while supplying essential interdisciplinary assumptions. Familiar concepts enter through a design-specific use, consequence, ambiguity, or test.

**Test.** Delete an explanatory sentence and identify the loss: necessary premise, distinctive mechanism, inferential step, or mere reminder. Apply the same test to headings, captions, and paragraph-end significance statements.

**Blocking failures.** Tutorial exposition, familiar principles offered as discoveries, elementary implications restated after the evidence, and language telling the reader what to recognize, remember, or conclude. Repeated 'this paper', 'this article', or 'the present analysis' framing is limited to genuinely useful methodological disclosure and the compact ensemble context.

**Evidence.** The specific work a retained explanation performs, or the repaired transition after its removal. The human paper's accessibility arises through concrete activities and places while its claims retain a scholarly method.

## 6. Gate G3 — Inheritance, syntax, transitions, and cadence

**Requirement.** Except at a deliberate turn, a sentence receives an actor, object, state, image, condition, term, or unresolved implication from its predecessor and transforms it. The same requirement applies across paragraphs and sections. Syntax expresses the actual causal, temporal, constitutive, comparative, concessive, or evidentiary relationship.

Coordination serves equal-weight propositions. Subordination, apposition, relative clauses, periodic development, and variations in sentence length serve actual dependencies. Passive voice remains available when it supports meaning and continuity. Short sentences can be earned hinges or landings.

**Test.** Trace inherited and transformed material sentence by sentence, including the clauses within long sentences. Check openings, subjects, rhythm, emphasis, breath, and paragraph landings. Test whether transitions arise from the subject matter. Read each section continuously after its argument has been repaired.

**Blocking failures.** Repeated subject-verb resets, equal-weight declaratives strung together, list-like clauses disguised by syntax, stock transitions, and compulsory concluding lessons. Mechanical sentence length or connective-density targets are excluded.

**Evidence.** Boundary-specific inheritance repairs and actual cadence findings. Major transitions, the conclusion, and difficult dense passages retain the standing read-aloud requirement. A silent review is recorded as silent; absent audible review remains pending, never silently credited.

## 7. Gate G4 — Affirmative scholarly stance, vivid action, and economy

**Requirement.** Develop claims through the subject, evidence, and conditions under which they hold. Integrate competing explanations where their premises enter. Qualifications attach to the affected claim. Maintain precise nouns and verbs and visible organisms, people, activities, institutions, physical processes, and consequences wherever they belong.

**Test.** Inspect defensive framing, anticipatory apologies, objection catalogues, promotional assertions, self-praise, generic importance claims, decorative adjectives, inert abstraction, and inflated contribution verbs. Check the specifically excluded authorial phrases `not` and `rather than`, then examine other negative-contrast structures semantically. A word substitution cannot repair an unchanged defensive argument.

Self-reference, rhetorical questions, reader-management, repeated summaries, and 'imagine how wonderful' instructions receive the same review in abstracts and captions as in the body. Recurring images or lexical threads must deepen through their new context.

**Blocking failures.** Persuasion through hype, caveat walls, elementary claims recast as breakthroughs, abstraction displacing organisms or people, and a technical qualification vanishing during stylistic editing.

**Evidence.** Located findings and dispositions, including verb and human/living-presence reviews. Preserve exact bibliographic titles, quotations, code, and mathematically necessary expressions. Any required exception in authorial prose is explicit and subject to author resolution.

**Dream adaptation.** Sensory and emotional force comes through prospective physical scenes, living relationships, recognizable activities, and meaningful choices. No invented completed future history, named future witness, universal cultural outcome, or unsupported sensory specificity. The prose can evoke desire without predicting that every reader or future resident shares it.

## 8. Gate G5 — Technical meaning, evidence status, and discriminating tests

**Requirement.** Editorial changes preserve assumptions, mechanisms, physical budgets, biological scope, geography, time horizons, system boundaries, and uncertainty. Separate sourced observations, original calculations, conditional models, design proposals, speculative possibilities, and fictional archive material.

**Test.** Check changed claims against their origins and downstream uses in every paper, figure, equation, and table. Every proposed study identifies what question it discriminates, its alternatives, fixed assumptions, measured outputs, and the result that would alter the design.

**Blocking failures.** Favorable assumptions from incompatible scenarios combined into one result; energy-limited and thermal pathways double-counted; radiated power confused with total civilization power; geometric air volume treated as usable living capacity; low-gravity mechanical scaling promoted to life-cycle viability; a drawing promoted to an ecological result; cultural opportunities presented as inevitable customs; numerical reproducibility described as physical validation.

**Evidence.** Claim/assumption register, semantic change record, model inputs and output versions, and question-to-test mapping. Feasibility arguments can remain conditional if the conditions and demonstrated content are exact. Full construction validation is not a prerequisite to publishing a properly bounded conceptual or numerical study.

## 9. Gate G6 — Full-document source admission

**Requirement.** Every final cited external source has an accessible complete local original, exact version, checksum, completed full-document reading record, quality/scope assessment, and claim-level page or section locators. Reading includes appendices and the source's supplied bibliography. This does not create an infinite requirement to read all works in every source's bibliography unless their claims are separately used.

Scholarly research and primary technical, measurement, or institutional records form the admissible evidence base. Wikipedia and discovery snippets do not enter the scholarly bibliography. Genuine primary release/interface records can establish what they actually document; their evidentiary role is narrower than a validated research result.

**Test.** Follow each important claim to the exact archived version and supporting passage. Check the surrounding qualifications, study population, experimental environment, and inference boundary. Review opposite or limiting findings where they bear on the argument.

**Blocking failures.** Abstract-only or selected-passage reading passed off as full access; browser access alone substituting for the required local original; inherited bibliography labels treated as fresh verification; inaccessible sources silently waived; citations to an earlier assistant report presented as external validation.

**Evidence.** Source manifest, full-reading record, claim ledger, and original files. A blocked source must be acquired or its dependent use changed through a substantive revision. A disclaimer leaves the admission issue unresolved.

## 10. Gate G7 — Exact-build visual and production review

**Requirement.** On later manuscript delivery, compile the agreed LaTeX sources and identify the exact PDF by hash. Inspect every page at readable scale, then figures, tables, equations, captions, arrows, labels, units, margins, pagination, links, fonts, and author metadata closely. Complex or previously problematic vector figures receive a second-renderer check.

**Blocking failures.** Compilation success, text extraction, contact sheets alone, an old build's images, or an unspecified PDF used as visual clearance. A final edit that changes layout reopens the affected checks.

**Evidence.** Page-specific observations, rendered pages, renderers and versions, source identity, and PDF hash. Scientific diagrams distinguish illustrative layouts from computed results. Generated scene images never stand in for model output or observed organisms.

This gate is deferred in the present planning-only delivery; no manuscript PDF has been created.

## 11. Required separate prose passes

The seven gates retain all ten standing operational passes:

1. Bead-string / equal-weight-declarative pass.
2. Sentence and paragraph inheritance pass.
3. Sentence/paragraph reorderability pass.
4. Verb and exact-action pass.
5. Human and living-presence pass, adapted to organisms and ecosystems as well as people.
6. Subject-generated transition pass.
7. Defensive-structure and affirmative-development pass.
8. Redundancy and expert-audience deletion pass.
9. Cadence pass, including honest distinction between silent and audible review.
10. Final-page arc pass: abstract, opening, and conclusion promise, develop, and land on the same contribution.

Conclusions gather developed lines into a new final relation or consequence. They do not replay the section sequence, recite the feature inventory, or deliver generic lessons. In the root, the ending returns the imagined experience to a credible relation with the present. In each companion, it lands on the conditions, contribution, and next consequential work established by the analysis.

## 12. Additional ensemble controls

### E1 — Distinct contributions and balanced whole-world scope

Each paper owns a question and produces something useful independently. Biology/ecology cannot become an incidental acceptance paragraph beneath engineering. Human life cannot collapse into a medical checklist. The root cannot turn into an engineering feasibility abstract. Equal importance does not require mechanically equal word counts.

### E2 — Vision-to-evidence and society-to-design traceability

Each prominent dream feature has a register entry: physical conditions, biological requirements, engineering support, human use, evidence status, alternatives, and failure conditions. Mark inherited archive concepts versus newly proposed variations. Atmosphere, vegetation, altitude, lighting, weather, and human activity in one scene belong to one compatible scenario.

Cultural ideas are plural design opportunities. Environmental conditions can enable festivals, settlements, work rhythms, and travel patterns without determining institutions, values, or individual preferences. Comparisons address documented mechanisms and scenario consequences; no invented future society is treated as a prediction.

### E3 — Shared parameters and reciprocal dependencies

One versioned register owns pressure/composition, water, carbon/nutrient inventories, gravity, rotation, filtered spectrum, regional environmental cases, construction horizon, and service horizon. Physical and biological requirements feed engineering; engineering outputs feed climate and ecology; human uses feed access and impact budgets. Engineering convenience cannot silently redefine the desired world.

Source locations, shield geometries, packet sizes, power mixtures, forest dimensions, and social arrangements remain alternatives until their evaluation justifies selection. Findings propagate through every affected manuscript, illustration, abstract, and root scene.

### E4 — Release integrity

Draft and review one section at a time with adjacent context. Repair argument first, then audience and movement. Revisit abstracts, transitions, captions, and conclusions after the body stabilizes. Follow local passes with a separate whole-manuscript challenge and a separate ensemble audit. The same reviewer can perform distinct passes, with their actual identity and activity recorded.

A release register uses PASS / BLOCKED / DEFERRED with exact artifact hashes and evidence. An automatic checker can establish record completeness and currency; substantive clearance requires actual judgment. Revisions reopen affected gates. No pre-filled PASS is permitted for work yet to be performed.

## 13. Boundary conditions to carry into research

The common objective is an open lunar atmosphere initially around 1.2 atm and a useful, diverse living environment, examined under a centuries-scale construction scenario and at least a billion-year maintained-service scenario. These horizons concern different questions. Biological establishment, forest maturity, engineering commissioning, and ecological persistence keep separate clocks.

The existing 17.5% O2 / 82.5% N2 design and literal Earthlike proportions remain separately labelled compositions. An approximately 250 K exobase is a candidate output to achieve. The 1.2-to-1.0-atm interval is a retention diagnostic. Whole-world success also requires water, elemental cycles, biological function, and supportable intervention.

Retain the native low gravity and slow solar cycle as core design context. Any proposed change in spin, orbit, lighting, gas mixture, or habitat enclosure must be explicit. The signature vision centers on open environments; local shelter and infrastructure remain compatible with it.
